Social Events Schedule


Tuesday, 2nd:

18:00 - 20:30: ACA 2013 Registration. Málaga Palacio Hotel. We will thank delegates to get their Conference pack at this moment.

21:00h: Welcome Cocktail. Málaga Town Hall. We will depart from Málaga Palacio Hotel at 20:45 (5 minutes walk). Málaga's Mayor will give a "welcome to Málaga" speech and later, we will enjoy the welcome cocktail. (Time could be slightly different due to Mayor's availability)

 

Wednesday, 3rd:

19:00 Málaga City Center Tour. We will depart from Málaga Palacio Hotel. We will walk around city center streets and monument and visit the Alcazaba (we will try to do a night inside tour). We will finish at "Pimpi" restaurant where we will enjoy the dinner with a flamenco show.

 

Thursday, 4th:

15:00 ACA 2013 Excursion: Granada and night visit to Alhambra de Granada. We will depart from Málaga Palacio Hotel by bus to Granada. After a bus visit to Granada, we will have dinner and later we will do a night visit to the Alhambra monument, a humanity patrimony building, which has been proposed to be one of the new seven marvels. After the visit we will return to Málaga.

 

Friday, 5th:

9:30: Extra excursion for accompanying persons: visiting Mijas, Benalmádena and lunch in a typical restaurant in the beach. Before and/or after the lunch, there will be time for a swim. Bus will be back at Malaga Palacio Hotel at 18:00.

21:00h: Banquet. Málaga Palacio Hotel.
