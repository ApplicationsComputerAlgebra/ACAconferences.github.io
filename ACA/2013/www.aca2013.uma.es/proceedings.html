Proceedings

Proceedings will be published with an ISBN. If your proposal is accepted, your abstract (you may also send, for publishing, to session organizers an extended abstract or short paper of up to 5 pages) will be included in the proceedings. The talk proposals (short or extended abstracts) will be reviewed by the session organizers and they will inform the authors about the acceptance. If acceptance was based on a proposal with short abstract, authors will have till May 25th to submit an extended version, up to 5 pages, to be included in the conference proceedings.

In the Conference package, you will get a book with the Proceedings. You can find a pdf version here.

 

Special Issue in JSC

In addition of the possible publications within special sessions, a Special Issue in the Journal of Symbolic Computation will be produced for the whole conference with the very best/top quality papers. Call for papers, deadlines and procedure will be announced next to the Conference days.
