Registration

 

Secure Online registration is now available with credit card (VISA, MasterCard, Maestro, Red 6000, Unicaja).

 

Registration fee for delegates:

Registration fee for conference delegates will cover admission to all presentations, Conference materials, lunches, coffee breaks, Tuesday’s welcome reception, Wednesday and Thursday excursions (with dinners), Friday's banquet dinner and discount prices for Thyssen and Picasso's Museums.
  • Standard, until May 25th: 325 Euros.
  • Late registration, after May 25th: 395 Euros.

 

Registration fee for accompanying persons:

Registration fee for accompanying persons will cover Tuesday’s welcome reception, Wednesday and Thursday excursions (with dinners), Friday's banquet dinner, an extra excursion on Friday morning and discount prices for Thyssen and Picasso's Museums.
  • Standard, until May 25th: 250 Euros.
  • Late registration, after May 25th: 320 Euros.

 

Procedure:

In order to register, please follow these instructions:

  1. Pay the registration fees (see below how to do it).
  2. Fill this registration form.
  3. Send attached the registration form to aca2013@ctima.uma.es. Please, refer to Registration of "YOUR NAME" in the subject line.
