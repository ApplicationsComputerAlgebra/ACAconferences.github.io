Title
Young Researchers Session
Organizers
Tateaki Sasaki <sasaki@math.tsukuba.ac.jp>
Stanly Steinberg <stanly@math.unm.edu>
Aim and Scope
The purpose of this session is to have young researchers that are doing excellent work on important problems present their latest results. The speakers are chosen by the Working Group of the ACA. The speakers may talk about any computer algebra related topic of their choosing.

If you know a person who will soon complete or recently completed a Ph.D and is doing excellent research, then please nominate them by sending me an email to both
Tateaki Sasaki <sasaki@math.tsukuba.ac.jp>
Stanly Steinberg <stanly@math.unm.edu>
indicating why you think your nominee should be chosen to talk in this session.

Please forward this announcement to anyone who may have a student, post doc, or young faculty member they wish to nominate.
