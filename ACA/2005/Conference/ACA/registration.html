Registration
One who want to participate ACA2005, please make the registration by an electric registration system (https://horner.math.tsukuba.ac.jp/ACA2005/registration/).
Conference fee
Early registration (until June 15) $200 (Yen 22000)
Regular registration (until July 15) $230 (Yen 25000)
Student registration $50 (Yen 5500)

The fee includes a conference bag with a Booklet of Abstracts, official coffees and cookies, Welcome Cocktail Party, and Sightseeing in Nara Park (July 31).
Sightseeing fees
Bus-tour to Houryuji Temple (Aug. 2, evening) $10 (Yen 2000)
Houryuji Temple is the world-oldest wooden made temple.
Bus-tour to Kyoto & Banquet (Aug. 3, afternoon) $20 (Yen 5000)
Visit several historical sites, such as Golden Temple, and Banquet at a Japanese-style House in Kyoto City.

The above fees are about 50-30% of actual costs (cheap !!).
