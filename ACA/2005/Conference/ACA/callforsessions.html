Call for Sessions
One who wants to organize a session must send a session proposal to Conference Chair Prof. M-T. Noda ( noda@cite.ehime-u.ac.jp ) by e-mail. The proposal should contain 1) session title, 2) session organizer(s) with an e-mail address for correspondence, and 3) spec and purpose of the session (10 - 20 lines).

The proposal will be reviewed by the International Advisory Committee and the proposer will get the answer within one week. The accepted proposal is taken to the ACA2005 Homepage promptly.

*** Deadline of Session Proposal: April 1, 2005.
*** Each session should contain at least 4 talks.
Instruction for Organizing a Session
Sessions are held in one or more two-hour blocks of time, each of which typically consist of four 1/2 hour talks or a one hour overview and two 1/2 hour talks.
These times include time for questions. It is preferable that sessions have two organizers, one experienced person and a younger colleague or student.

The duties of the session organizers are:
  • Submit a proposal for the session to the conference general chairs
  • Invite the speakers ( sample letter for invitation )
  • Maintain a web page describing the session with abstracts for the talks. Examples can be found on the ACA web page. ACA can assist you with the web page.
The scientific quality of a special session is the sole responsibility of the session organizers
