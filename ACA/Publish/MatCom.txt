Aim
---

This special issue of _Mathematics and Computers in Simulation_ (MatCom) will
contain selected papers from the conferences ACA'2001 (Albuquerque, NM, USA)
and ACA'2002 (Volos, Greece) that emphasize computer algebra applications.

Computer Algebra has found applications in many domains of science such as
Applied and Theoretical Mathematics, Computer Science, Biology, Medicine ... as
well as Engineering, Social Sciences ...  According to the MatCom web page:

   http://authors.elsevier.com/journal/matcom

this journal is devoted to an audience of "Simulation Specialists, Applied
Mathematicians, Statisticians and Systems Analysts".  Thus, some of the topics
explicitly covered by the journal are contained in the title of this special
issue.

Therefore, this CFP is directed to papers with a strong "applied" component.
It is suggested that authors of theoretical papers about Computer Algebra or
where a Computer Algebra System is only used at a certain step to perform some
auxiliary calculations, submit their papers elsewhere.

Please observe that the deadlines in this CFP are the same for those in the
Special volume of JSC (also) containing papers from ACA'2001 and ACA'2002.
Please avoid double submissions of the same paper.  It is up to the authors to
decide which of the special issues they wish to submit.

Calls for papers from JSC and this notice can be found at
   https://ApplicationsComputerAlgebra.github.io/ACAconferences.github.io/ACA/Publish/publish.html


Submission Guidelines
---------------------

Papers must be submitted to any of the guest editors.  They must be prepared
using the LaTeX Class File Elsart.cls, which can be obtained from

   http://authors.elsevier.com/journal/matcom

When using bibtex, use the file elsart_num.bst

   http://www.elsevier.com/locate/latex

The recommended paper length is 15 pages (using Elsart.cls)-- longer papers
must be justifed by the importance of their content.

Electronic submissions are encouraged, and may be sent as one email (MIME
attachments are allowed).  The message should contain
(i)  the abstract in ASCII and
(ii) the whole paper in Postscript.
The Postscript form must be interpretable by Ghostscript, and must use standard
fonts, or include the necessary fonts.  Authors who cannot meet these
requirements should submit 5 hard copies by post instead.

All submitted papers will be refereed according to the usual refereeing
process.

To help in estimating the number of papers that will be submitted, we would
appreciate an email expressing the intention to submit a paper (including
author information, a tentative title and abstract, and an estimated number of
pages) as early as possible.
