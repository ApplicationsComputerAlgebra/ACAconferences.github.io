Meals

Lunch:   12 - 14 €

Vitosha Park Hotel enjoys quick and easy access to Sofia International Airport, city center, etc. 

 

 

For booking rooms at the above prices

please write e-mail message (it is still possible ) to the following address:

reservations@vitoshaparkhotel.com

with subject  ACA2012 .

Content of the message:

  • Full name of the Conference participant

  • Arrival day

  • Departure day

  • Single or double room is requested

  • Preferable methods of payment 

    • Bank transfer in advance (data for invoice)

    • By credit card (Visa, MasterCard, AMEX)

    • In cash on arrival.

Food restrictions will be accepted on arrival.
