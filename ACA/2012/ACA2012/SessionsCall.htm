Call for Special Sessions

  

The ACA meetings usually are organized as a series of Special Sessions.  ACA 2012 will run in this standard format.

 

Individuals can propose a special session by sending an E-mail to the Program Chairs by 29 February 2012. The Scientific Committee (i.e. the ACA working group)  will decide on the acceptance of the proposed sessions. The accepted proposals will be announced on the Conference web site.

 

A Proposal for a Special Session has to include:

·     title of the Special Session,

·     short abstract describing the Special Session,

·     contact information of the organizers.

For more details see http://math.unm.edu/aca.html .

 

The talk submissions have to be directed to the organizer of

an appropriate special session.
