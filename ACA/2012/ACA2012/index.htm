The ACA series of conferences is devoted to promoting the growing applications of computer algebra in science, engineering and education. They provide a forum for researchers, developers and users of computer algebra algorithms and systems, and for anyone interested in computer algebra applications.

The 18th ACA Conference will continue this tradition.
