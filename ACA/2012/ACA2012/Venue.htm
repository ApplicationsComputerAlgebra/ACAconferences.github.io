Venue

ACA 2012 meeting will take place in Sofia, the capital  and the largest city of Bulgaria. It is located at the foot of Vitosha Mountain in western Bulgaria. Interesting historical, geographical and cultural features of Sofia are described, for example, at:

en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia

www.youtube.com/watch?v=2oUwgGLiz0c

The ACA 2012 sessions will run in Vitosha Park Hotel located in the south area of Sofia, at just 10 minutes drive from the city centre. It is situated among a beautiful park, not far from the foot of Vitosha Mountain.

The conference rooms of the hotel are equipped with computers,  projectors and presentation screens.

Accommodation of the conference participants in the same hotel is provided.

Local Organizer of the Conference is the Institute of Mathematics and Informatics at the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences.
