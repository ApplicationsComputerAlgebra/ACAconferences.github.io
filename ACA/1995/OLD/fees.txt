Registration Fee (New Reduced):
        Members of IMACS
                On or before May 1, 1995 - $225 USA
                After        May 1, 1995 - $275 USA
        Non-members add $25

The registration fee was reduced on April 1 because we now have a better
estimate of the number of attendees and have consequently moved the
conference to smaller facilities (still on the University campus).
For those who have paid the higher fee already, refunds will be made
at the conference.

Registration fees can by paid by Visa, MasterCard, American Express or
Diners Club cards, institutional check, personal check, money order,
purchase order, wire transfer (all fees must be paid at point of origin)
and cash (on-site only please).  Make checks payable to ACA-IMACS.  Mail
payments to Sandbar Productions, P.O. Box 3824, Albuquerque, NM 87190.
