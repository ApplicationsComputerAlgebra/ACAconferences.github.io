Some announcements regarding the IMACS Conference on Applications of Computer
Algebra:

Please send in your registration forms even if we are paying your registration
fees!!!  This information is important for our record keeping.

More information, such as maps, local sites, and so forth have been added to
both the WWW pages (http://math.unm.edu/aca.html) and the anonymous FTP area
(ftp://math.unm.edu/pub/aca).  The latter has a file called INDEX detailing the
files collected there.  These areas will remain active as the conference
approaches.

The WWW page for the electronic proceedings/preliminary program organized by
sessions and the anonymous FTP file talks.txt will keep track of talk titles
and speakers.  In the next few days, a preliminary conference program will be
produced (in PostScript).  An announcement will be sent out when the program
becomes available.

We are trying to organize some group activities for some of the lunch periods
and evenings for any who wish to participate.  Walks around campus and nearby
areas (especially Sandia Crest which has an elevation of 10,378 feet) are
under consideration.  We will let you know more when the program comes out.

If you wished to be removed from further mailings for this conference (but not
future conferences), please send a note to aca@math.unm.edu .

take place starting at 8 am, Tuesday, May 16, 1995 on the University of New

Central Avenue is also known as Route 66 of TV fame.  This was the main
east-west highway used by anyone traveling from the East to California.

If you are coming from Hokona Hall (for those staying in the dormitories), walk
south from Hokona a dozen feet or so until you reach an east-west sidewalk.
Follow the sidewalk west past the Education building (on the left) and the
Business School (on the right), and then turn south on the long north-south
sidewalk that starts where the east-west sidewalk ends.  This will lead to the
stairs on the north side of the plaza (see map).  The building to the south is
the SUB.  Now, that was easy, wasn't it? ;-]

AIRPORT -> HOTELS

A free shuttle is available between the airport and the conference hotels
(Hilton Hotel and Fairfield Inn).  In theory, the shuttle comes by the airport
hourly, but one can simply request it by using one of the courtesy phones in
the baggage claim area (these are well marked with the name of the hotel
listed).  There are shuttles to other Albuquerque hotels as well.

DORMITORY ROOMS

Those attendees arriving by plane and staying in the dormitories should take
a taxi from the airport.  The approximate cost is $6-7.  Tell the taxi driver
that you want to go to Hokona Hall on the University of New Mexico campus.
Hokona Hall is located on Campus Boulevard, directly south of the University of
New Mexico Hospital on Lomas Boulevard.

Hokona Hall consists of two pentagonal "lobes" connected by a short hallway.
The dorm rooms will be in the Zuni section of Hokona Hall which is the western
lobe.  To check in, you will need to go to the lobby in the the central
hallway.  From the street, ypu will see two blue canopy covered entrances.  Go
*past* them around the side of the building (in either direction) until you
come to a door marked LOBBY.  If the door is locked, there is a buzzer you can
press to fetch someone to let you in.  The rooms will be available Monday night
(May 15) through Friday night (May 19).  Check-in time is 11 am to 10 pm.  If
you arrive outside those times, there are phones and instructions for calling
someone who will check you in.  The standard check-out is by 11 am on Saturday
(May 20).  If you wish to stay until Sunday, please make arrangements to do
this when you check in.  If you are arriving before the dorm rooms are
available, or leaving after the dorm rooms are no longer available, you will
need to arrange for a hotel room for those times.

There was a mistake on the dorm prices.  Each room is $23.  Thus, if you are in
a room by yourself, the cost will be $23 per night. If you share a room, then
the price will be $11.50 per night.  Should this mistake cause you any
difficulty, please contact Professor Steinberg.

The dorm occupants should plan on purchasing breakfast and dinner at one of the
many restaurants on Central Avenue.  These are very near the meeting site.

PARKING

There is a visitor parking lot southeast of the Student Union Building (see
map).  The cost is $3/day.  If you are staying in the dorms, you can obtain
daily passes there for $1.50/day.  There is also scattered free parking in the
streets south of campus, however, you must avoid posted areas that require a
parking permit.
