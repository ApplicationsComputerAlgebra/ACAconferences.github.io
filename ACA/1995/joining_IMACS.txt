                 Joining IMACS or IMACS-TCSC

                            IMACS

One can join IMACS by contacting one of the Secretariats and requesting an
application.  Dues are $30 US a year.  This does not include subscriptions to
any of the IMACS journals, which can be obtained at greatly reduced prices
with a membership. In addition, members pay reduced registration fees at IMACS
sponsored meetings.  For more information,
        email: imacs@cs.rutgers.edu

          TECHNICAL COMMITTEE on SYMBOLIC COMPUTING

One can join the IMACS Technical Committee on Symbolic Computing by using
WWW (mosaic) to fill out a membership form accessible from the ACA URL:
        http://math.unm.edu/aca.html

The main activity of this group is to organize the Applications of Computer
Algebra meetings.  Dr. Michael Wester will be in charge of this technical
committee, with Prof. Stanly Steinberg assisting, until the group has its
first formal meeting at the 1995 Applied Computer Algebra Meeting in
Albuquerque.
