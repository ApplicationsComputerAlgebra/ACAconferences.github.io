We will publish the proceedings as a hierarchal electronic document available
via the World Wide Web.  Anything from an abstract to a full paper is
acceptable.  In all cases, the paper should reference relevant contributions to
the field.  The deadline for submissions is July 1, 1995.

Papers submitted MUST be in one of the following forms: 
	ASCII text
	single PostScript file
	completely self contained collection of TeX/LaTeX/AMSTeX/AMSLaTeX files
	working URL pointing to a document
To aid in the construction of the electronic document, we will request a
separate file be provided with the paper's title, author's name and email
address, and a list of keywords for the paper.  Michael Wester, Conference
Coordinator, will be organizing this document.

In addition, parts of the proceedings for the meeting will be published as
special issues of IMACS journals.  The organizers of closely related special
sessions should submit a proposal to Stanly Steinberg, Conference Chair and
Guest Editor.  Note that if the total number of pages for an issue exceeds 200
pages, then only selected papers will appear in the special issue.

The journals available are:
	Mathematics and Computers in Simulation (Elsevier)
	Applied Numerical Mathematics (Elsevier)
	Journal of Computational Acoustics (World Scientific)
