Session: Engineering education

Title: Computer Algebra and Interpolation: a Lesson Plan

Abstract:

Polynomial interpolation and related numerical analysis questions can
be used to take users of computer algebra systems from issuing
one-liners to writing more complex programs. An alternate use of the
outlined lessons is to introduce students to interpolation and
acquaint them with splines and numerical differentiation. Constructive
symbolic derivations of formulas for polynomial interpolants are
presented. Numerical differentiation formulas can easily be derived
from them. The examples and assignments can be done using any general
purpose symbolic manipulator. Solution codes using the computer
algebra system AXIOM will be outlined.
