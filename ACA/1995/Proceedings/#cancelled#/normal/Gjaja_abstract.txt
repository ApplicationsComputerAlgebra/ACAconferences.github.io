  Some estimates for Normal Form Transformations Performed with
                 a Composition of Lie Transforms
                           Ivan Gjaja

                  Center for Nonlinear Studies, 
                 Los Alamos National Laboratory,
                               USA
                   e-mail: ivan@swan.lanl.gov

    The problem considered is the normal form transformation of a 
Hamiltonian system where the unperturbed motion is linear and the 
perturbations  bring in a finite number of Fourier  harmonics  at 
each order.  The transformation is effected through a composition 
of Lie transforms generated by homogeneous polynomials. Estimates 
are obtained for the norm of the generating functions and for the 
minimal  remainder.  The results are compared with the  estimates 
for the remainder obtained using other methods.
