          Notes on Transcendent Cases of the Stability
                     Andrzej J. Maciejewski

    Liapunov stability of an equilibrium of a Hamiltonian  system  
with  two  degrees of freedom is determined  by  coefficients  of  
Birkhoff  normal  form.  A  transcendental case  occur  when  all  
coefficients whose sign decide on stability vanish.
    Symbolic  normalization up to higher an higher terms  is  not 
decisive  in such a case.  I consider such cases that appear  for  
low order resonances and prove that transcended cases  considered 
can  appear  only  when the equilibrium is  degenerated  in  some 
sense: it lies on a manifold of equilibria or specific  invariant 
manifold.  I give examples of mechanical systems where such cases 
appear.
