Algebra for the Electronic Marketplace

This talk will explore formal structures that characterize automated  
mechanisms for exchange using market-generated prices. The goal of  
such formalization is the development of "smart" market mechanisms  
that employ algebraic means to optimize and coordinate the allocation  
of goods to market participants "on the fly." Research results for  
both centralized and distributed market systems are discussed.  
Differences from neoclassical views of markets as static  
surplus-maximizing mechanisms are highlighted.
