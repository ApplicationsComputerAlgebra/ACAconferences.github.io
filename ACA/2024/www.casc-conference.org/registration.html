Conference Registration


Please fill online registration form here

CASC 2023 Registration Fees are as follows:

- Academic: 300 €
- Student: 200 €
- Accompanying person: 100 €

Registration fees include:
- coffee breaks
- social program, excursion
- conference dinner

Payment is possible by cash only (payable in euros, US, or Canadian dollars), at the conference registration desk.
There is no early/late registration fee.
In order to enter Cuba with a valid passport, participants are advised to obtain an ETA (Electronic Travel Authorization) document from the website https://www.dviajeros.mitrans.gob.cu/inicio by applying online ahead of their trip.
