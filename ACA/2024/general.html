General Session at ACA'2024 to be held June 24-28 at the University of Havana, Cuba

Organizers:

Michael Wester, University of New Mexico, USA

Overview:

Computer algebra topics not covered in other sessions.

If you are interested in proposing a talk, please send an abstract to Michael Wester. Please use this LaTeX template for your abstract and send both the LaTeX source and a compiled PDF version. We suggest that abstracts be at least half a page including references.

Talks

  1. Completing Parametric Unimodular Rows to Unimodular Matrices
    (Dingkang Wang and Xiaopeng Zheng, China)
  2. Algebraic Analysis of Zero-Hopf Bifurcations of Polynomial Differential Systems
    (Bo Huang, China)
  3. Computing the greatest common divisor of several parametric univariate polynomials via generalized subresultant polynomials
    (Hoon Hong and Jing Yang, USA/China)
