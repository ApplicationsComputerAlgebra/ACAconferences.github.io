Panel Discussion at ACA'2024 to be held June 24-28 at the University of Havana, Cuba

Organizers:

Jing Yang, China

Overview:

The panel discussion at ACA 2024 aims to convene a distinguished group of researchers and practitioners to exchange knowledge and insights on pivotal topics within the realm of computer algebra. The scope of the panel will encompass a variety of subjects, including but not limited to:

  1. Application Trends: Engage in a dialogue about the most recent advancements, emerging trends, and groundbreaking innovations shaping the field of computer algebra and its practical applications.
  2. Multidisciplinary Perspectives: Elicit a spectrum of viewpoints by assembling a diverse panel of experts from varied disciplines, backgrounds, and specialized domains within computer algebra, ensuring a comprehensive discourse.
  3. Future Directions: Project and deliberate on the prospective trajectories and burgeoning areas that are poised to influence future research and development in computer algebra.

Invitation to Participants:

We extend an open invitation to all ACA 2024 participants who are eager to contribute their perspectives on these topics and related areas. Join us for an engaging and enlightening discussion that promises to foster new connections and spark innovative ideas.
