Social Events

  • Pre-registration and Welcome Reception, at ÉTS, Wednesday, June 24th, 2009 from 5pm to 7pm.
  • Three different activities in Montréal are offered for Friday, June 26th, 2009. (see details of excursions). When you register for ACA 2009, do not forget to indicate which excursion you'd like to take, if any, and your excursion fee will be added to your registration fee.
  • The banquet dinner will be held on Saturday, June 27th, 2009 at the restaurant, Auberge Saint-Gabriel, in Old Montréal. (see menu). The restaurant is a 15 to 20-minute walk from ÉTS and is situated at 426 Saint-Gabriel St.
    The cocktail preceding the banquet dinner will start at 6:30 pm. Cocktail and dinner will take place in the Grenier room of the Auberge. Sorry, the banquet room is not wheel-chair accessible.
    Vegetarian menus or menus for those with food allergies are available. Do not forget to indicate any special needs of this sort when registering.

For those persons accompanying delegates, please see the Info-Montréal page to help you prepare for your visit to Montréal.

The magnetic card that will be included with your conference documents will give delegates access to ÉTS's Sports Center locker rooms and showers. Bring your towel as well as a lock.

For any questions you may have concerning the social aspects of ACA-2009, please contact Kathleen Pineau.
