  General information about the conference. This information will be available in the paper version of the program, which every delagate will receive upon their arrival at the conference.

The ACA series of conferences is devoted to promoting the applications
and development of Computer Algebra and Symbolic Computation.
Topics include computer algebra and symbolic computation in engineering,
the sciences, medicine, pure and applied mathematics,
education, communication and computer science, etc.
