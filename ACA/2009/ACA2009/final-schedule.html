A Welcome Reception will be held Wednesday, June 24th, from 17:00 to 19:00. A conference desk will be set up in the lobby of building B (1111 Notre-Dame street West) where delegates can pick up their conference documents and get information. Delegates will then be invited to join their colleagues at the University Pub for cocktails.
These documents will also be made available on Thursday, June 25, from 7:45 to 10:45, near Nortel room A-1600, during the Welcome ceromony. For the rest of the conference, the conference desk will be in room B-4418 of building B.
