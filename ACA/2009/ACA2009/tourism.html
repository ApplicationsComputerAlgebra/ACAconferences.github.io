 		 Visit École de technologie supérieure (ETS)
  • How to get to ETS
  • Take a virtual tour
  • Recommended Hotels

ETS is located in downtown Montréal, 20 km from the city's international airport. A taxi fare from the airport to downtown costs approximately 38$. If you are arriving by car, paid parking is available at most hotels. Montreal's Central Train Station is located under the Queen Elizabeth Hotel, within easy walking distance of ETS.
