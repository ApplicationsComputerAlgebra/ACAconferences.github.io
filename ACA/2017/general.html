General Session on Computer Algebra

at ACA'2017 to be held July 17-21 at the Jerusalem College of Technology, Jerusalem, Israel

Organizers:

Michael Wester, University of New Mexico, USA
Stanly Steinberg, University of New Mexico, USA

Overview:

This session is for talks that do not fit into any of the other ACA sessions. All proposals in the scope of the conference are welcome.

If you are interested in proposing a talk, please send an abstract to the main organizer. Please use this LaTeX template for your abstract and send both the LaTeX source and a compiled PDF version. If you do not work with LaTeX, the submission may be sent in Word format (.doc or .docx file) and later, once accepted, we will try to adapt the proposal to the LaTeX template.

    (Edgardo Cheb-Terrab, Maplesoft R&D, Canada)
  2. The four double-hypergeometric Appell functions, a complete implementation in a computer algebra system
    (Edgardo Cheb-Terrab, Maplesoft R&D, Canada)
  3. The International Mathematical Knowledge Trust
    (Ingrid Daubechies, Patrick Ion and Stephen M. Watt, USA and Canada)
  4. How a code for verifying our conjecture opened new directions
    (Eli Bagno, Jerusalem College of Technology, Israel)
  5. Using Gröbner basis theory for an interval method solving undetermined equations
    (Bartłomiej Jacek Kubica, Warsaw University of Life Sciences, Poland)
