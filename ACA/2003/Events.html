ACA 2003
 

July 28-31

ACA 2003 Events

Tuesday, July 29, 2003

The ACA 2003 Banquet will be held on Tuesday night at the NCSU University Club (4200 Hillsborough), about 3 miles West along Hillsborough from Harrelson.

  • Special Buses will run to and from the banquet on Tuesday as follows:
    • 3 bus run from North Hall Residences to the University Club: 6pm, 6:30pm, 7pm
    • 3 bus runs the University Club to the North Hall Residences: 9:30pm, 10pm 10:30pm.
  • Cash bar 6:15pm.
  • Dinner 7:30pm.

 

Wednesday, July 30, 2003

A special tour of Red Hat, the purveyors of Red Hat Linux and undoubtedly the premier Open Source software company in the world. The headquarter is located on the North Carolina State University Campus.
  • Buses will leave around 8:30am and will be back in time for the first talks at 11am
  • More information to come!

 

Thursday, July 31, 2003

A trip to Wrightsville Beach, along the beautiful North Carolina Coast.

  • Buses will leave after the Panel Discussion at 10:30am
  • Bring sunscreen, white clothes and a hat!
  • The cost of the trip is included in the registration fee.
    • Accompanying persons should be able to get seats, but because we are not able to run extra buses, we can't yet make a guarantee. Such requests will be considered at the conference.
