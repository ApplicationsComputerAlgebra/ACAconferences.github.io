ACA 2003 Locations

All ACA 2003 Sessions will take place on the first floor of Harrelson Hall on the NCSU Campus (Rooms HA100, 107, 113 and 147)

The banquet on Tuesday night is at the NCSU University Club (4200 Hillsborough), which is about 3 miles West along Hillsborough.
Buses will run to and from the banquet on Tuesday as follows:

  • 3 bus run from North Hall Residences to the University Club: 6pm, 6:30pm, 7pm
  • 3 bus runs the University Club to the North Hall Residences: 9:30pm, 10pm 10:30pm.
  • Cash bar 6:15pm. Dinner 7:30pm.
