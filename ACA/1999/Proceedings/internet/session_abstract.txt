Title: Mathematics on the Internet

Abstract:

The subject of the session is the general problem of putting
mathematics on the Internet.  A non-exclusive list of topics is:

   -  placing math formulas in web documents
   -  attaching semantic meaning to expression trees
   -  standardizing the representation of expression trees/graphs
      and procedures that generate mathematical objects or
      that apply them to inputs (black box math objects)
   -  transmitting math objects across the web
   -  brokering services with math objects between servers and
      clients
   -  standardizing programming interface and hierarchy
      of math objects
   -  standardizing math character encodings
