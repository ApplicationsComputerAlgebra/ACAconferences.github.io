Session Description:

The session aims is to provide a forum for discussing new trends in
combinatorial and computational methods in algebraic geometry, in particular
their applications in outstanding problems like the Jacobian conjecture,
Embedding conjecture, Nagata's conjecture and Cancellation conjecture in affine
algebraic geometry.

Session Topics:

Groebner Bases and Applications
Locally Nilpotent Derivations and Applications
Primitive Elements, Retracts, Test Elements and Automorphic Orbits
Exotic Algebraic Structures on Affine Spaces
Jacobian Conjecture
Embedding Problem
Nagata Conjecture
Cancellation Problem
