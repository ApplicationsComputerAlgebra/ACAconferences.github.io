Session Description :

The interaction on physics, number theory and computer science is an old one. The recent progress of symbolic computation on Feymann diagrams, knot invaviants and chord diagrams leads to calculation of transcendental numbers (multiple zeta values, harmonic series, ...) and the need for new algorithms on algebraic methods. The software developments and their intensive experimentations lead to very deeper conjectures on number theory.

The aim of this session is to present several examples of how computers can be very useful for obtaining new results, new conjectures and ... new mathematics, via the following topics

Session Topics

  • ACA in High Energy Physics, Quantum Field Theory, Statistic Physics,
  • ACA in G-Functions, Hypergeometric Functions, Polylogarithms,
  • ACA in Harmonic Series, Multiple Zeta Values,
  • ACA in Hecke Algebra, Quasi-Hopf Algebra, Quasi-Symetric Functions,
  • ...
