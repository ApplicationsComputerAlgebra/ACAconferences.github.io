Resume:
  This session will focus in how Computer Algebra affects Mathematics
  teaching, in particular how it affects the Spanish educational system.
  For instance, now there are alternative ways to introduce classic
  specific contents in the classroom.  Moreover, with these new technologies,
  new objectives can now be reached and new contents can be taught.
