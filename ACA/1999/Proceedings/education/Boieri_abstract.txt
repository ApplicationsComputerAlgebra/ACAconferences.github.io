Exploring sequences with DERIVE

Sequences usually are not treated as deeply as functions in Calculus courses.
However, sequences show many interesting properties that do not appear in
elementary presentation of functions.  This paper describes a teaching unit
that, using DERIVE, helps the student of a first year University course in
Calculus to discover and understand some features of the behaviour of a
sequence, defined as a function of n or by recursion.  Several computer
experimentations introduce the problem of understanding the idea of how fast a
sequence grows for n going to infinity and what is the meaning of comparing
sequences behaviour at infinity.
