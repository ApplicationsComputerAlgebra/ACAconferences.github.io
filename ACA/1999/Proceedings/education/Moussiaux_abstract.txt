CONVODE: A Reduce package for solving differential equations

CONVODE is a REDUCE package that resolves ODE, PDE, systems of ODE, PDE, ...
The use of CONVODE is very simple. The package is constructed on classical
methods for finding solutions and also to keep its user aware of the solving
process. By design, CONVODE is not a "black box" providing a solution without
comments. In the opposite, we know the way followed by CONVODE to provide a
solution. We do not claim that this way is always the best but we want also
preserve the "pedagogical"' look of our package for use by students. Convode
may also be used via E-mail without problems,see:
http://www.physique.fundp.ac.be/physdpt/administration/convode.html
