   Title:     Toric Ideals and Integer Programming
   Organizer: Lorenzo Robbiano (Universita' di Genova, Italy)

   The purpose of this session is to discuss the interaction
   between classical problems in integer programming and new methods
   coming from computer algebra.

   Potential speakers (so far):
   L. Robbiano, A. M. Bigatti,  R. Hemmecke,  S. Hosten
