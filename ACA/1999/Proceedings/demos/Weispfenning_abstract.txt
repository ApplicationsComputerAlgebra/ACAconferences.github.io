REDLOG-Demo at IMACS-ACA'99 


Title:             The REDLOG Package
                - Tools and Applications

          Volker Weispfenning, University of Passau

REDLOG is a REDUCE-package developed by Andreas Dolzmann and Thomas Sturm at
the University of Passau, Germany.  It puts a very flexible first-order logic
environment on top of the computer algebra facilities of REDUCE.  Special
emphasis is on efficient elimination algorithms for real numbers, algebraically
closed fields, and valued fields.  REDLOG has numerous applications in
computational geometry, automatic theorem proving, solid modeling, motion
planning, control theory, simulation of networks, and optimization.

The demo will outline the facilities and tools of the package with examples
from these application areas. 

For more information consult the REDLOG homepage:

http://www.fmi.uni-passau.de/~redlog/
