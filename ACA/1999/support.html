ACA'99 Financial Support

Depending on grant funding, some support for organizers and attendees may be possible. Any individual interested in assistance should send email to
imacs-aca99@ccedu.ucm.es
giving their name, the session they will organize or in which they will present. They should include a budget for a specific amount (in US dollars of Spanish Pesetas) they will need to attend the meeting (separate amounts for airfare, lodging, registration, etc). They should also give a brief description of their interest in computer algebra, e.g. I am a student studying quantifier elimination or I am a full professor and have an extensive publication recorded in Groebner basis problems.
