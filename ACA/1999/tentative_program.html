More About the Tentative Program:

Documentation will be picked up at the reception desk of the residence.

5 parallel sessions are expected to take place at the same time (except the 27th after lunch, when there will be 4 parallel sessions).

English is the official language of the conference. The education session will have simultaneous translation English<-->Spanish and presentations in Spanish will be accepted (to confirm).

Keynote Lectures (1 hour)(preliminary titles):

The following invited speakers have confirmed their willingness to give a 1 hour talk at the conference (as in previous IMACS-ACA, there will be no plenary lectures, and these talks will take place within normal sessions).
  • Victor Edneral (Moscow State Univ., Russia)
    Title: Computer Algebraic Approximations - Creation of Approximated Solutions of Scientific and Engineering Problems in Symbolic Form
  • Richard Liska (Czech Tech. Univ. Prague, Czech Republic)
    Title: Finite Difference Numerical Modelling Supported by Computer Algebra
  • Tomas Recio (Univ. de Cantabria, Spain)
    Title: Missing points and branches of real parametric curves
  • Stanly Steinberg (Univ. New Mexico, USA)
    Title: The Study of Stability using Computer Algebra
