A proceedings volume with the abstracts of the papers presented at ACA'2002 will be published and will be available at the conference.
The deadline for submissions of abstracts of the talks is set for April 20, 2002.
Participants should submit their abstracts in text or LaTeX form to session organizers.
Session organizers are responsible for submitting all the abstracts they have received, before April 20, 2002.
This April 20 deadline is an absolute deadline, imposed by our sponsor.


Selected full papers from those presented at the conference as well as at ACA'2001,
will be published at a special issue of the Journal of Symbolic Computation.
The deadline for submissions of full papers is set for December 30, 2002.
The title of the special issue is:

Special Issue on Applications of Computer Algebra.
