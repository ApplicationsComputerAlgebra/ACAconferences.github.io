Session on Numeric-Symbolic methods in Physical Modeling
Organized by Ioannis Emiris, André Galligo, Ilias S. Kotsireas

in the frame of the conference

ACA'2002, June 25-28, 2002, Volos, Greece





This session is intended to discuss, without being limited to,

  • interactions of numeric and symbolic methods in computer algebra,
  • applications to geometric modeling.
    • Research contributions as well as expository papers are solicited. For more information and/or to present a paper at the session, please contact the session organizers by e-mail.

    List of talks:
    1. Adam V. Adamopoulos, Michael N. Vrahatis, Spiridon D. Likothanassis, Democritus University of Thrace, University of Patras, Prediction rules in chaotic binary data
    2. Laurent Busé, University of Nice, Solving polynomial systems with residual resultants
    3. Ioannis Emiris , INRIA Sophia-Antipolis, France, Implicitization of rational parametric hypersurfaces
    4. André Galligo , University of Nice, France, Self intersections of bicubic surfaces
    5. Ilias S. Kotsireas , ORCCA-WLU, Ontario, Canada, Efficient implicitization of curves, surfaces and hypersurfaces with applications to CAGD.
    6. G.C. Meletiou, T.E.I. of Epirus, Michael N. Vrahatis, University of Patras, Handling Cryptographic problems through Interpolation, Approximation and Neural Network Techniques
    7. Victor Pan , CUNY, New York: Polynomial Root-Finding by Exploiting Matrix Structure and the Eigenvectors
    8. Jean Pascal Pavone, University of Nice, France: Self-intersection of general parameterized surfaces
    9. Olivier Ruatta, INRIA Sophia-Antipolis: Application of computer algebra techniques to implicitization of rational surfaces with base points.
    10. Agnes Szántó, University of Kent, U.K. Solving over-determined systems by subresultant method

    Papers presented at the session will be considered for publication at a special issue of the Journal of Symbolic Computation titled Applications of Computer Algebra. The call for papers is available. Check out the ACA'2002 Important Dates.
