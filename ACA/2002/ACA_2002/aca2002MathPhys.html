Session title: "Computer algebra applied to equations arising in mathematical physics and control theory" The goal of the session is to provide a forum for developers of constructive algebraic methods and software for equations and systems of interest in mathematical physics and control theory and for those who needs them to investigate and solve particular equations. The session topics include: - group analysis of differential and difference equations - blow-up regime and power solutions - linear systems of partial differential equations - integrability analysis of nonlinear evolution equations - completion of polynomial and differential systems to involution - computations with quantum algebras and groups - ordinary and partial control systems - constrained dynamics and differential-algebraic equations - simulation and stability of mechanical systems Session organizers: Vladimir Gerdt Laboratory of Information Technologies Joint Institute for Nuclear Research 141980 Dubna Russia Tel.: (+7-09621) 63437 Fax: (+7-09621) 65145 E-mail: gerdt@jinr.ru Alexander Prokopenya Brest Technical University Moskowskaya st., 267 224017 Brest Republic of Belarus Tel.: (+375-162) 422643 Fax: (+375-162) 422127 E-mail: prokopenya@belpak.brest.by
