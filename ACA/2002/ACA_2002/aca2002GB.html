Session on Gröbner Bases and Applications
Organized by Elizabeth Arnold, Patrizia Gianni, Ilias Kotsireas

in the frame of the conference

ACA'2002, June 25-28, 2002, Volos, Greece





This session is intended to discuss recent results in Gröbner bases as well as applications of Gröbner bases to other fields. Research contributions as well as expository papers are solicited. For more information and/or to present a paper at the session, please contact the session organizers by e-mail.

barnold@math.tamu.edu     gianni@dm.unipi.it     ilias@orcca.on.ca
