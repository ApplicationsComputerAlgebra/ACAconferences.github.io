In this work,   fourth-order partial divided-difference equations  satisfied by the bivariate Racah and the  bivariate Wilson polynomials are derived. From our result, we recover the difference equation satisfied by the bivariate Racah polynomials given by J.S. Geronimo and P. Iliev. Moreover, we make a conjecture on the form of the partial divided-difference equation satisfied by any multivariate Racah or Wilson polynomials. To illustrate this conjecture, we consider the case of the trivariate continuous Hahn polynomials.
