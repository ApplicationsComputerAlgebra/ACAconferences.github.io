General Session on Computer Algebra

at ACA'2016 to be held August 1-4, 2016 in Kassel, Germany

Main Organizer:

Michael Wester, University of New Mexico, USA

Overview:

This session is for talks that do not fit into any of the other ACA sessions. It is organized by the ACA'2016 program committee. All proposals in the scope of the conference are welcome.

If you are interested in proposing a talk, please send an abstract to the main organizer. Please use this LaTeX template for your abstract and send both the LaTeX source and a compiled PDF version. If you do not work with LaTeX, the submission may be sent in Word format (.doc or .docx file) and later, once accepted, we will try to adapt the proposal to the LaTeX template.

Talks

  1. Computation of Hilbert Schemes
    (M. Albert, University of Kassel, Germany)
  2. Noether Normalization and Involutive Bases
    (Amir Hashemi, Isfahan University of Technology, Isfahan, Iran)
  3. Divided-difference equation and three-term recurrence relations of some systems of bivariate q-orthogonal polynomials
    (D. D. Tcheutia, M. Foupouagnigni, I. Area, E. Godoy and Y. Guemo Teffo, Germany, Cameroon and Spain)
  4. Linear partial divided-difference equation satisfied by multivariate orthogonal polynomials on quadratic lattices
    (D. D. Tcheutia, Y. Guemo Teffo, M. Foupouagnigni, E. Godoy and I. Area, Cameroon and Spain)
  5. Efficient computation of the bivariate chromatic polynomial for special graphs
    (M. Gerling, University of Kassel, Germany)
