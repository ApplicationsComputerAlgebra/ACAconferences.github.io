1998 IMACS Conference on Applications of Computer Algebra

Financial support

Note that all attendees, including organizers and speakers, will be responsible for all of their expenses including registration, travel, meals, and lodging. However, there is some modest support available. Applications should be sent to aca98@siduri.fjfi.cvut.cz and should include requested amount and purpose. Preferences will be given to students, junior faculty, organizers, and speakers.
