Scope:

The meeting will focus on actual or possible applications of nontrivial computer algebra techniques to other fields and substantial interactions of computer algebra with other fields.

Meeting Format:

The meeting will be run in the standard IMACS format where individuals are invited to organize a special session. Individuals can propose a special session by contacting the program chairs. All paper submissions must be directed to an organizer of an appropriate special session, which will be listed at the web sites.
