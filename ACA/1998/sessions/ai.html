IMACS-ACA (Applications of Computer Algebra) conference has taken place for three times now (1995-New Mexico, 1996-Austria, 1997-Hawaii). General proceedings are published in electronic form.

At the 1996 and 1997 ones I organized a session entitled "Non-Standard Applications of CA". Some of these articles are collected in a 1997 special volume of the journal Mathematics and Computers in Simulation (Transactions of IMACS) that will appear very soon.

I'm organizing a session entitled "Computer Algebra Systems Applications to Artificial Intelligence" at 1998 IMACS-ACA (to take place at Prague, Czech Rep.; 9-11 August 1998).
