Convex hull

Graham's algorithm is an algorithm to compute the two dimensional convex hull of a given finite set of points. The instability of the algorithm derives from the predicate whether a point is to the left or to the right to or directly on a line. This predicate has zero discontinuity. We applied the stabilization techniques to the algorithm and experimented on an input sample of 3,000 points, with success. In this application, in some cases one can verify the correctness <#308#>visually<#308#>.

However, as far as the two dimensional convex hull problem is concerned, in most cases a naive floating-point computation method seems to be sufficient as well.

The stabilization techniques would be more effective for higher dimensional cases or other problems in computational geometry.

We refer the reader to [#PS##1###] for example for the details of Graham's algorithm and other topics in computational geometry.
