We have explained that zero rewriting is done preceding evaluating predicates. However, in implementation, it is often more convenient to perform zero rewriting after every function evaluation. This extra rewriting was done for the Buchberger and Sturm algorithms. This is good in the case where a predicate does not explicitly appear in the description of an algorithm. For example, the function of taking the lead term from a polynomial implicitly involves a predicate whether a coefficient is zero.
