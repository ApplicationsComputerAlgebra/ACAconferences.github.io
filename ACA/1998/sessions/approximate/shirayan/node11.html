We do not mean to indicate that it is a wrong direction or unimportant to pursue the precision problem.13 For some algorithms, it may be very important to perform stochastic analysis on a large number of experiments regarding precisions, considering the worst case, the average case, and the best case. Is the stabilization methodology useless as long as the precision problem is unsolved? Our answer is NO, and in the next two sections we propose new directions in which one may make the best of the stabilization methodology.
