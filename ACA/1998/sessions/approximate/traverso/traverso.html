Computing Gröbner bases numerically:
some experiments

Cinzia Bonini, Klaus-Peter Nischke, Carlo Traverso1
Dipartimento di Matematica, Via Buonarroti 2, I-56123 PISA
e-mail:{bonini,nischke,traverso}@posso.dm.unipi.it

The paper reports some ideas and an implementation for the computation of a Gröbner basis using floats from either exact or inexact data. In this implementation we use hybrid coefficients, i.e. coefficients composed of a float (a bigfloat) and an integer mod p.

The main parts of the paper are the following:

  • outline of previous and current research in this domain;
  • description of the hybrid arithmetic and its implementation;
  • use of numerical strategies in Buchberger algorithm for exact input, a variant modeled over the lazy tangent cone algorithm;
  • inexact input, the complete intersection case;
  • inexact input, the over-determined case;
  • alternatives to hybrid computation: intervals, guard bits;
  • experiments.

The aim of this research is manifold:

  • To challenge the assertion that the numerical computation is unsuited for Buchberger algorithm;
  • To investigate numerical strategies in the Buchberger algorithm;
  • To have indications on the feasibility of a purely numerical Buchberger algorithm.

The hybrid arithmetic and some numerical pivoting strategies have been implemented in the FRISCO polynomial system solving C++ library and framework. We report the results of some preliminary experiments, and of some planned improvements to the implementation that will speed-up the computation.
