"Parallel poly-pushdown automatic groups"

G.Baumslag
City College of CUNY
Math Department, NAC, Convent Avenue at 138th Street
New York, NY 10031
USA
E-mail:gilbert@groups.sci.ccny.cuny.edu



Abstract: Parallel poly-pushdown automatic groups are
generalisations of automatic groups, designed to capture
some aspects of computations that can be carried out in 
parallel. This allows for computations with groups which
are not necessarily finitely presented. The notion is
the result of joint work of the speaker with Hamish Short and Michael
Shapiro.
