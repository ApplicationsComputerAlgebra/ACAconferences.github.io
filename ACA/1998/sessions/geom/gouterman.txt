"Graded  mappings  of  Frobenius  type  and  their   algorithmic
 constructions".

Alexander Gouterman.
Department of Mechanics and Mathematics, Moscow State University



     ABSTRACT.

     In the proposed work a clasification of one-to-one, linear,
even  mappings  of  the  tensor  product  spaces   over   graded
superalgebra, which preserves tensors of rang one, is  obtained.
Let $F$ be a graded superalgebra, $U$ and $V$ be  graded  spaces
over $F$. If mapping $T$ on their tesor  product  has  aforesaid
properties, it can be represented as tensor product of to linear
bijective mappings $A: U\to U$  and  $B:V\to  V$.  The  mappings
matrices are constructed algorithmically.
