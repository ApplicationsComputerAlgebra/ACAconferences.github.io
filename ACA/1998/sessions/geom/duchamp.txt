Computation of minimal idempotents in the Hecke algebra:
a Schutzenberger-Gelfand-Zetlin approach.

G.Duchamp, University of Rouen, France


Abstract: 
After a small presentation of Schutzenberger's
combinatorial heritage, we focus on recent results concerning
the computation of q-analogues of Young's idempotents.
We give also a fast algorithm to evaluate minimal Jucys-Murphy
idempotents in the Hecke algebra.
