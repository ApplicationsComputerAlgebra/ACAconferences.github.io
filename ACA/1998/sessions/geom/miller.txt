"An Alternate Method for Computing SAGBI-Groebner Bases over a PID"
(preliminary report) 

William W. Adams, 
University of Maryland, 
Serkan Hosten, 
George Mason University, 
Philippe Loustaunau, Systems 
Planning and Analysis, Inc., and 
J. Lyn Miller*, Western Kentucky University.

Abstract:

We present developments arising from the search for an alternate 
technique for constructing SAGBI-Groebner bases (analogs of Groebner 
bases for ideals of subalgebras of a polynomial ring) in a polynomial 
ring over a principal ideal domain.  This technique avoids such heavy 
use of additional Groebner basis computations as were given in an
earlier paper (1996) by Miller.
