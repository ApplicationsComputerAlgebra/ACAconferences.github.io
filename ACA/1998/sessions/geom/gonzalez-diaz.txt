"An approach to computing the action of Steenrod squares
 on the cohomology of polyhedral simplicial sets"

Rocio Gonzalez-Diaz, Pedro Real Jurado
Univ. de Sevilla, Spain.
e-mail: real@cica.es


 Abstract: A combinatorial formulation for Steenrod squares is given
in [Gonzalez-Diaz R. and Real P. : ``A combinatorial method for
computing Steenrod squares''. To appear in Journal of Pure and Applied
Algebra (June, 1999) ] .  This computer algebra tool is used here in
order to derive several results concerning the action of the Steenrod
algebra on the cohomology of polyhedral simplicial sets.
