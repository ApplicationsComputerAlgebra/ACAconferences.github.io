"Determining coordinates for polynomial algebras"

Jie-Tai Yu
The University of Hong Kong, Hong Kong.
e-mail: yujt@hkusua.hku.hk


Abstract:

p\in K[x1,..,xn] is called a coordinate if p is an automorphic image
of x1, or, equivalently, there exisit p2,...,pn in K[x1,...,xn] such
that K[x1,..,xn]=K[p,p2,...,pn].

In this talk, we will consider the problem how to determine whether
p\in K[x1,..xn] is a coordinate and relevant topics.  In particular,
when n=2, we are able to solve this problem completely by two
different methods.
