"Linear recurring sequences over rings and modules"

A.V.Mikhalev* and A.A.Nechaev, Moscow State
University, Russia.

Abstract:

The aim of the talk is to extend some fundamental and applied results
of the theory of linear recurring sequences over fields to the case of
polylinear recurring sequences over rings and modules.
Quasi-Frobenius modules and Galois rings play a very special role
in this project.
