"Complex Application of CA / ICS in High-Tech Engineering Education"

* L. Drska, R. Liska, M. Sinor
  Faculty of Nuclear Sciences and Physical Engineering
  Czech Technical University in Prague


This contribution discusses the following topics related to the 
application of Computer Algebra (CA) and Integrated Computing Systems 
(ICS) in higher education : (1) CA / ICS as a key tool in high-tech  
research and education of exact sciences and modern engineering. (2) The 
use of CA / ICS as a part of a complex application of information 
technology in education. (3) Specific requirements on the CA / ICS 
packages to be used in physics and engineering curricula. (4) Particular 
application of CA / ICS at the FNSPE CTU, information on selected courses 
in information sciences and introductory / advanced physics. (5) Plans for 
future application of CA / ICS in education at the FNSPE CTU, their role 
in the development of the virtual department of physical sciences and 
information pedagogy. The full text of the presentation will be available 
at the URL http://www-troja.fjfi.cvut.cz/~drska/edu/aca981/.
