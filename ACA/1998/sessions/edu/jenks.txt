"How To Create Textbooks With Live Mathematics Using LATEX"

Richard D. Jenks
IBM Research
P.O. Box 218
Yorktown Heights, New York   10598
USA
E-mail:jenks@watson.ibm.com


This reports a study of designs and techniques for creating live 
mathematics from markup in new or existing mathematical 
documents written in LATEX. We present LATEX extensions to techexplorer 
that provide both presentation and semantic 
content markup suitable for transmission to computer algebra systems using 
OpenMath and/or MathML.
