"Introducing and implementing CAS into 
 pre- and in-service education and training"

Yasar Ersoy
Middle East Technical University (METU)
Dept SSME, Middle East Technical Uni.
06531 Ankara
Turkey
E-mail:yersoy@tutor.fedu.metu.edu.tr


Much attention has been directed at reforming school mathematics. Some 
projects in various countries are working on the integration and 
implementation of information technologies, namely computers and 
calculators, into school mathematics curricula and undergraduate 
courses in universities. The central theme of these projects is the 
use of technologies to enhance the learning of mathematics. Because, 
computer software tools such as computer algebra system (CAS) and 
graphical packages provide an ideal environment for student 
exploration of multiple representations, i.e. the connections between 
the visual/graphical representation and the analytical/algebraic one. 
In the present paper, we summarize our experiences in and issues on 
introducing and implementing information technologies, namely 
computers, CAS and TI-92 in the pre-service and in-service education 
and training of mathematics teachers in Turkey.
