"Application of computer algebra systems in educational 
process in Novosibirsk State University"

Nina Kalinina


Algebraic computation programs have alredy been applied 
to a large number of different areas in science and engineering. 
At use of systems of computer algebra it is possible to select 
two directions, namely: first - application computer algebra system  
at study of general mathematical courses and, second use computer 
algebra system at preparation of the students,learning computer science. 
In the report versions of application of systems of 
computer algebra as in general mathematical courses, 
and in special courses in Novosibirsk State University 
are considered.
