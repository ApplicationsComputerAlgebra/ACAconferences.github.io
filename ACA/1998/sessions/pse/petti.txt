
      "Perspectives on integrating mathematics and software"

                            Richard Petti
                            Macsyma, Inc.

I will cover two topics in my talk.

First I will briefly discuss how Macsyma Inc. combines Macsyma 
(symbolic and numerical math in Lisp), Lapack (numerical linear 
algebra in Fortran), PDease2D (finite element analysis in Pascal) 
and a notebook interface (in C++).  This package includes multi-
engine on-line help that supports several software packages in 
the same user interface.

Secondly I will discuss some ideas on how to optimize intellectual 
returns on investment in mathematics, using concepts from strategic 
and organizational analysis.  First we identify some underlying 
"intellectual technologies" in mathemtics, and distinguish between 
those technologies which are relatively mature and those whose basic 
enabling technology is advancing very rapidly.  Organizational 
concepts of effectiveness and competence provide some perspectives 
on the pursuit of academic excellence in mathematics. This line of 
thinking suggests some strong conclusions about the relationship 
between conventional computational mathematics, modern abstract 
mathematics and computerized computation.
