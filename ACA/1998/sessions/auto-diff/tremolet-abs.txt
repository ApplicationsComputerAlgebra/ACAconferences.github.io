"Adjoint parallel code"

Yannick Tremolet 
Environmental Modeling Center
National Center for Environmental Prediction
W/NP2, WWB, Room 207
Washington, DC 20233


Adjoint models are the basis of variational data assimilation both
in meteorology and oceanography. Since these applications require a
lot of computational power and memory, in the past few years many
models have been developed for distributed memory parallel computers.
We will present the methodology used at NCEP to derive the
adjoint of the parallel spectral model which was written using a
message passing library.

We will describe the impact of the parallelism in the code on the 
derivation of the adjoint and show how to obtain the adjoint of a code
containing explicit message passing instructions, including point to
point communications and group or global communications. We will then 
present the automatic differentiation tool Odyssee and how it was
possible to use it for our purpose. Finally, we will give some  
validation results and performances of the derived code.
