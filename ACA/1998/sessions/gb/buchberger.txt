    "Computer Support for Inventing Groebner Bases Theory"

Abstract: We will speak about the Theorema project (based on Mathematica),
which aims at supporting mathematicians in playing with new notions and
studying their consequences. The main tools are various computer-supported
provers that generate proofs in a style that imitates human proving. The
project is motivated by the author's work on various variants and
generalizations of the Groebner bases theory.
