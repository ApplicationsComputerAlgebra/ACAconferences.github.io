Optimzing the process of production of crystals for LHC

Serge S. Bityukov
Moscow State University, Russia
serge@shade.msu.ru

Abstract

The problem of optimal planning of linear multistage process of production of identical objects is considered. To obtain the solution, a mathematical model is introduced and explored. The results obtained can be used reduce the amount of combinatorial caculations needed to find the optimal phase trajectory. The program based on these results is presented.
