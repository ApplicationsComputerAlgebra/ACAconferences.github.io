
"Polynomial System Solving for industrial problems:
 A report from the FRISCO project"

Laureano Gonzalez--Vega
Departamento de Matematicas, Estadistica y Computacion,
Facultad de Ciencias, Universidad de Cantabria, Santander, Spain.
E_mail: gvega@matesco.unican.es


In this talk the FRISCO (A Framework for Integrated Symbolic/Numeric
Computation) project will be presented together with the
activity developped inside the project towards the transfer, to companies,
enterprises and R\&D laboratories, of the last achievements in polynomial
system solving.

This will be done by presenting, at least, two concrete examples arising,
the first,  from the design of a transistor and, the second, from the
CAD/CAM software in an industrial framework. In these examples it will be
shown how the use of new techniques for Polynomial System Solving has been
very useful to solve the real-world problems considered.
