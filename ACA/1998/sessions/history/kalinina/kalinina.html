The Kantorovich Schemes as base structures of realization of algebraic transformations in early of computer algebra systems 1

Nina A.Kalinina
Institute of Informatics Systems, Novosibirsk,Russia
e-mail: kalinina@iis.nsk.su

March 30, 1998

Abstract:

The form of representation of algebraic expression is discussed known as Kantorovich scheme and used broadly for solving computer algebra problems. Modified Kantorovich schemes variants are analyzed. The ways of using Kantorovich schemes for parallel performing of symbolic transformations are discussed. The advantages of Kantorovich schemes include possibility to apply transformation rules to algebraic expressions and to perform independently and easily set of scheme strings.
