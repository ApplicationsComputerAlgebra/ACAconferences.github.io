The Tree Representation. The Schemes Received at Different Rounds of the Tree

The structure of algebraic expression is not in its essence linear. The tree representation of expression presents full information about syntax structure of the expression and clearly points out linkages between its elements.

Ab initio KS represented the tree of expressions as a sequence of numerated strings, where the first element of a string was the operation of this string and other elements were the operands of the scheme.

The KS may be received from Kantorovich graph. This graph is resembles data flow diagram. The different directions produced different schemes.

For example, for the expression 

    EX = (v*w + v**2 + 9) * (2*cos(v) + 7)

the Kantorovich schemes may be next.

For instance, 

Scheme 1
T1 =V*W      T2 =V**2     T3 =T1+T2     T4 =cos(v)
T5 =2*T4     T6 =T5+7     T7 = T3+9     EX =T7*T6

Scheme 2.
T4 =cos(v)   T5 =2*T4     T6 =T5+7      T1 = v*w
T2 =v**2     T3 =T1+T2    T7 =T3+9      EX = T7*T6

and at last by directing from level to level we recieve 

scheme 3:
T1 =v*w      T2 =v**2     T3 =cos(v)    T5 =2*T4
T3 =T1+T2    T6 =T5+7     T7 =T3+9      EX = T7*T6
