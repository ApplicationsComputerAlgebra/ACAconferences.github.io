Modified Schemes in Computer Algebra Systems

As it was mentioned in Section 1, the KS's are often used for elaboration of both special and general systems. Most completely properties of these schemes were used in such systems as ANALYTIC [2], [3] and the system for analytical differentiation [4].

Certain applications require special KS modifications. For instance, AUM system KS modification is constructed on the base of appropriate using of both binary and n-order KS.

KS-modification used in [4] is based on certain similarity of approach to expression translation, considered as transformation of expression according to rules which allow to receive resulting translation form with the process of differentiation considered as strictly fixed process of expression transformation according to the well-known rules of differentiation. Applied modifications were chosen according to their efficiency for given transformations and the background of realization.
