Kantorovich Schemes as a Base for Formulas Representation for Symbolic Manipulation

The range of formula representations in Computer Algebra Systems (CAS) is rather broad. Different variants of formula representations are usually based on different principles.

The principle of universal approach to performing heterogeneous transformations would appear more fruitful. A successful example of using this principle was the use of Kantorovich Schemes (KS) as a form of formula representation in computer algebra systems. Such form was offered by L.V.Kantorovich as soon as the development of computer technologies inspired hopes for their broad applications [1]. This form appeared useful for various applications [2]-[5]. Today this form is supposed to be fairly promising for parallel performing of algebraic transformations.
