Experience of Computer Algebra Using
in Perm State University

Vladimir V.Malanin, Igor E.Poloskov
Perm State University,
Bukirev st. 15, Perm, GSP, 614600, Russia



polosk@psu.ru

Abstract:

Below there is considered a history of using and development by the Perm State University employees of computer algebra systems (CAS) for solving of various educational and scientific tasks.

The works connected with using of computer algebra systems and separate programs have begun and are continuing during more than twenty years in the chair of Mechanics and (processes of) control and in the CAS Laboratory of Perm State University (Perm SU) under leadership of V.V.Malanin.

The beginning of these works was stimulated by occurrence at our university in 1975 of the minicomputer Mir-2 with the built-in programming language Analytic-71 for analytical calculations and in 1976 of "large" computer ES-1020 which was compatible with IBM/360 and had a rather various for that time software.

Using new technologies I.E.Poloskov has constructed the first (in Perm SU) two applied programs of symbolical manipulations intended for realization of the A.A.Krasovskiy iterative method, method for solving of the Fokker-Planck-Kolmogorov equation (FPK Eq.). The first program used the CAS Analytic-71 (1976) and the second was realized as a package of procedures on the base of the programming language PL/1 (1976-77). The tasks of the package were the following: to input symbolical representations of the FPK Eq. coefficients, to built powers of the FPK Eq. differential operator (with use of known rules of differentiation and predetermined substitutions) and to generate PL/1-procedures for numerical account of these powers with optimization of a target text.

Approximately in the same time (1977-78) M.U.Drozdov started to exploit the Analytic-71 system for a building of motion equations for an object involved several rigid bodies.

It is natural that these first attempts have not allowed to reach some significant result (due to system and program restrictions) but have shown necessity and importance of computers symbolical opportunities use. In fact during this period we did not suspect about an already existing rather wide spectrum of various purposes CAS and accordingly had not any literature about them.

Gradually a study of abstracts journals has resulted that we have found out that in problems of CAS and their usage there were engaged seriously in the Joint Institute of Nuclear Researches (JINR, Dubna). Via one of our university physicists, which was in JINR many times due to scientific missions, V.I.Lumpov succeeded to establish contacts with employees of JINR Laboratory of Computer Techniques and Automation (LCTA). As a consequence the following stage of computer algebra usage in Perm SU was connected with an installation of universal CAS Reduce-2 on ES computers in 1979 (with assistance of the LCTA of JINR employees V.P.Gerdt, V.A.Rostovtsev and R.N.Fedorova with which V.I.Lumpov and M.U.Drozdov worked in close contact during significant time). It has allowed to begin a development of problem oriented program packages on the base of CAS Reduce for applications in theoretical mechanics (V.I.Lumpov [1]), including for an analysis of random processes in nonlinear dynamic systems, and a derivation of equations of a relative motion for systems of rigid bodies and a generation of Fortran-4 programs for calculation of equations right hand sides (M.U.Drozdov [2]).

A creation of more refined programs was promoted by an installation of the Lisp and RLisp software (1980) and the CAS Formac-PL/1 (1981) which were given by LCTA of JINR under a contract about cooperation. Developments of V.I.Lumpov and M.U.Drozdov and also I.E.Poloskov work on realization of statistical dynamics methods with the help of CAS Formac achieved rather quickly a level which has allowed to participate not only as listeners in different CAS conferences but also to represent own reports (International meetings in JINR since 1982, conference in Gor'ky, 1984 etc.).

Further in Perm SU there were introduced the systems Al'cor and Vibran (1985) given by the authors L.V.Provorov and G.P.Kul'vetis. When PC became accessible we started to use the CAS muMath-muSimp (1989), Mathematica 1.1 (the system was received by V.V.Malanin at Oxford university under a contract about cooperation in 1991) and Derive (1991). Since 1995 the CAS Mathematica 2.2 and Maple V3 are exploited and since 1997 Mathematica 3.0 and Maple V4 are.

During performance of works with CAS using new young employees (G.Shul'gina, V.M.Mikriukov, O.Szukov, L.S.Starostina, A.E.Bajanov etc.) were included in such works. About 1982 under a leadership of V.M.Suslonov within the framework of similar theme there was generated the subgroup of employees (R.Munipov, V.N.Ivanov, l.N.Bulatnikova, A.B.Bjachkov) used CAS Reduce for a building of mechanical systems motion equations in the Lagrange form, with the use of quasi-coordinates etc. The special CAS Laboratory was created as a subdivision of the chair of Mechanics and (processes of) control. Later employees of other Perm SU divisions have become interested in questions of CAS applications. During some time the university seminar discussing CAS from various points of view worked.

Let us list the main directions of CAS using in various Perm SU divisions which have developed for past years:

1#1 training the students

- lecturing of some general and special courses connected with CAS structure and applications for students of mathematics, mechanics and applied mathematics;

- preparation of annual and degree works, final works of bachelors and master dissertations;

- building of the computer courses on theoretical mechanics (V.M.Mikriukov) and on statistical dynamics (I.E.Poloskov);

- development and performance of individual tasks;

- publication of the educational and methodical literature;

1#1 scientific researches

- automation of dynamics modelling for mechanical systems including connected rigid and elastic bodies (on ES computers and PC) and solution of special tasks in this sphere (M.U.Drozdov, Reduce);

- realization of the iteration operator method for FPK Eq. solving and building of ordinary differential equations (OD Eqs.) right hand sides (r.h.s.) for the power series method, construction of interactive shell for work with the CAS Reduce (on ES computers) from within shared systems (V.I.Lumpov);

- realization (on ES computers and PC) of different methods of a random effects in nonlinear dynamic systems investigation, automatic generation of Fortran and Pascal subroutines for neglection and calculation of OD Eqs. r.h.s. for coefficients of FPK Eq. solution expansion into a series, automation of vector-matrix expressions processing during a derivation of motion equations with differentiation (if it is necessary) of these expressions with respect to parameters (I.E.Poloskov, Formac, Reduce, Mathematica, Maple);

- development of a programs package (ES computers, PC) expanding CAS Reduce by tensor operations (V.M.Mikriukov);

- deriving of motion equations for mechanical systems in the Maggi form (A.B.Bjachkov, Mathematica);

- realization of approximate methods and proof of constructive theorems in the theory of the functional differential equations (A.N.Rumjantsev, the chair of Economic cybernetics, Formac, muMath-muSimp, Maple);

- realization of the oscillatory functions method for differential and integral equations solving, problems of general theory of relativity (the chair of High mathematics, Reduce);

- quantum-chemical accounts (V.Szarkov, the Institute of Natural Sciences, Mathematica);

- derivation of the electrochemistry equations, solution of equations in complex variables (the chair of Physical chemistry, Mathematica);

- accounts in hydrodynamics (the chair of Theoretical physics, Reduce);

- long symbolic manipulations during difference schemes building (the chair of Applied mathematics, Derive)

etc.

During a fast development of works concerned with a range of CAS problems we had steady scientific communications with the employees of many organizations. Among them, except already mentioned LCTA of JINR, it is necessary to note the Institute of mechanics problems of USSR AS/RAS, the Institute of mechanics of Moscow University (M.V.Grosheva), the Institute of applied mathematics of USSR AS/RAS (G.B.Efimov), the Computer Center of Siberian Branch of USSR AS (Irkutsk), the Institute of theoretical astronomy of USSR AS/RAS etc.

Unfortunately in the beginning of 90-th in connection with general situation in Russia the majority of our scientific developments (including with application of computer algebra) was closed down. Due this reason many our employees have changed places of work and the significant part of scientific contacts has appeared lost. It is necessary to hope that symptoms of restoration of an interest to a science and CAS in our country (especially with support of the RFFR) appeared recently will fill the suffered losses in any measure.
