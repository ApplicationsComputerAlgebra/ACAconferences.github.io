Possible applications of CA Methods in Ecology

Nikolai K. Zaitsev
Gubkin National Academy of Natural Oil and Gas
Moscow, Russia
demis@econics.msk.su

Abstract

The technical methods of quantitative monitoring of the environment including the analytical chemistry and physical measurements are available in principle. However so far there is a strong lack of corresponding mathematical models to process these data and make decisions. The present abstract is devoted to some possible directions of modelling, applied to ecology and nature protection.

First, there exists a problem of a transformation of multiple and essentially heterogeneous data into a limited set of sum parameters which allow administrator to make decision. For that one should take into account both excesses and deficiencies of the special parameters or contents of different substances, their relative toxic activity and the limits of endurable values.

Second, the dynamic model of the propagation of the pollutant is important. Along with the evident type of propagation, called landscape scattering, one should take into account the concept of so-called landscape geochemical barriers which provide a very specific type of the distribution of a pollutant.

The next group of models can be the models of definite objects or processes, which are important and for which there is no definite concept with a final prognostic power: the ozone layer, the mass exchange at the surface of Ocean, at the interface the bottom/water, or greenhouse effect. Naturally, there are some empiric or theoretical models for such systems, but the CA could provide the frameworks for such models to be developed and improved finally to fit the reality. A universal model of a landscape including several trophical chains could be highly appreciated.

And the last group of models are the models of analytical instruments and the artificial systems for environmental purification. In one of the abstracts we tried to model a wide range of electroanalytical instruments widely used in environmental monitoring.
