   "The Automatic Investigation of the ODEs of Nonlinear Elasticity"


   Peter Vafeades
   96 Egnatia
   54623 Thessaloniki Greece
   Email: peter@perseus.engr.trinity.edu


   ABSTRACT: Any constitutive law model for nonlinearly elastic materials
   gives rise to a series of nonlinear boundary value problems. The most
   elementary and interesting problems give rise to ordinary differential
   equations which are solved automatically. The symmetry properties of the
   model are used to set up boundary value problems whose geometry allows
   for the greatest application of Lie group methods. The investigation
   includes issues of  stability and bifurcation as well as conservation
   laws and  the thermodynamic implications of the solutions.
