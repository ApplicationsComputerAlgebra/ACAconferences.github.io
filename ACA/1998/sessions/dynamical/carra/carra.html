Abstract:

A procedure is shown in order to study a special class of ordinary nonlinear and nonalgebraic differential equations. In fact ordinary differential equations, that are polynomials in the derivatives and in some elementary functions of the derivatives are considered. A procedure it is shown in order to transform such equations in algebraic differential equations by using some differential elimination procedures.
