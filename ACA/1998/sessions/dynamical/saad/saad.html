Symbolic computation of the periodic solutions
of the Barbanis-Contopoulos system
by the Lindstedt-Poincare method

Benbachir Saad
University of Rabat,
21 Rue Helsinky Appt 1, Ocean, Rabat, Morocco

Abstract

In this paper we will seek the periodic solutions of the Barbanis-Contopoulos non integrable hamiltonian system. We will use the Lindstedt-Poincare method in order firstly to enumerate the six main periodic families in the neighbourhood of the origin (one "horizontal", two "oblique", one "curvilinear" and two "circular"). Then we will determine the series representing these families as well as their periods. The calculation of all the series will be done to the order O(A21) by means of the computer algebra system "Mathematica", where A is a zero-th order parameter. We will also show that the well-known celestial technique of "elimination of secular terms" is rigorously equivalent to the "Fredholm alternative". We will moreover prove that the period of the two "oblique" periodic families is exactly equal to a Gauss hypergeometric series. We will further study the convergence of the series representing the periods using an accelerating of the convergence algorithm. The validity of the periodic families will be tested by a numerical integration. Finally, we will compare our results with those of the "geometrical" method of Churchill-Pecelli-Rod and we will particularly point out the failure of this last method concerning the research of the "circular" periodic orbits.
