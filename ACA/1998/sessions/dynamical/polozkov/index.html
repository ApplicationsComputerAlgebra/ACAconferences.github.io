Computer Algebra and Random Analysis Algorithms

Igor E.Poloskov
The Perm State University,
Bukirev st. 15, Perm, GSP, 614600, Russia



polosk@mail.psu.ru

The present paper is devoted to the description of applications of computer algebra systems to problems of the random phenomena analysis.

Main directions of researches in statistical dynamics are connected with a solving of stochastic differential equations (Eqs.) governing systems phase vectors, the Fokker-Planck-Kolmogorov Eqs. for the probability density and the Pugachev integrodifferential Eqs. for the characteristic functions.

Nowadays there is a significant number of exact and approximate methods intended for solving of such Eqs. But to perform most of them time consuming mathematical calculations are required (transformations, reductions of similars, differentiations, integrations etc.). The advantages of the CAS Mathematica and Maple applications and a technique of a numerical & analytical calculations organization in this sphere are considered in the message.
