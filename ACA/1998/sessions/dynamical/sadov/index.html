Dense allocation of quasi-homogeneous polynomials
and computation of the Lyapunov focus quantities for
cubic systems

S.Yu. Sadov
Keldysh Institute of Applied Mathematics, Moscow, Russia

Abstract

The problem of finding the center conditions for general planar cubic systems may appear to be within the capabilities of modern computers, but exceeds the capabilities of existing general purpose symbolic software. We are concerned with the first stage of the calculation: the Lyapunov focus quantities (the second stage is Gröbner bases computations). The scheme described avoids a redundancy in memory and, probably, is optimal in time as well. The techniques includes: 1) enumeration and ordering of coefficients of polynomials whose terms' exponents satisfy two linear equations; 2) effective addressing algorithm (relating a term's exponent and the placement of the term's coefficient in memory).
