WARNINGS:

Prague is not wild east, so do not worry too much. We just believe that it is better to warn you about potential problems now so you can avoid having bad experiences during your visit here.

Pickpockets WARNING:

In downtown areas crowded by foreign tourists appear pickpockets. In such crowded areas and in public transportation crowded by tourists, e.g., tramway nr. 22 between castle and city center, take greater care of your pockets and bags. Their standard worldwide strategy is to attract your attention by any means and then steal something from you.
Taxi WARNING:
Unfortunately Prague taxi drivers are famous for cheating their custommers, especially foreigners, by asking higher (sometimes much higher) prices. We believe that it is better to warn you. Here are a few pieces of advice:

1.
agree on the price before entering the car

2.
never catch taxi just on the street

3.
price should be stated on the front doors of the car

4.
we suggest you to use either hotel Krystal taxi Tel: 316 27 65 or AAA Radiotaxi Tel: 1080; best is to phone them personally; this has several advantages:
everyone at their dispatching on Tel.: 1080 or Krystal reception Tel: 316 27 65 speaks English
you might ask them about the price; EVEN after that, however, agree with the driver on the price
for Krystal taxi you might check their prices when you arrive
AAA Radiotaxi claims to have no overcost troubles for rides ordered through their dispatching
for AAA you do not pay anything for their car to come
the AAA price is 17 Kc/km and 3 Kc/min for waiting; their prices from the airport and railway stations to the hotel Krystal are below; price for one ride between Krystal and city center (Venceslav square) is approx. 220 Kc


How to get to the conference site (hotel Krystal):

Address:

Hotel Krystal
J. Martiho 2/407
160 41 Prague 6 - Veleslavin
Tel: 316 27 65

Current exchange rate is 30.7 Czech crowns (Kc) per $. Exchange offices are located at the hotel, airport and railway stations. When exchanging money, keep the receipt which you would need for changing the remaining Czech crowns back.

1.
from the Prague Airport

Krystal is quite close to the airport--do not take shuttle to the center.

a.
by public transport

take right exit from the arrival hall; go straight approx. 40 m crossing a few taxi lines to the bus stop which is a bit on the right with transparent shelter; take bus number 119 from the Airport to the stop "Divoka Sarka" (7th or 8th stop - one stop is optional, only after a signal to driver) which is close to ending loop of tramway tracks; you need to go about 100 m straigth ahead; change to tram nr. 20 or 26; exit on the 2-nd stop "Koospol"; go straight ahead along the tracks 300 m and hotel Krystal (see photo) is on your right (see map of Hotel Krystal neighbourhood).

you have to buy the ticket at the airport before entering bus, either in automatic machines (you would need coins) or in a newspaper stand; buy a ticket for 12 Kc (valid for 60 min after stamping, in all city public transportation means); after entering the bus, stamp it in yellow stamping machine inside the bus

cost - 12 Kc, estimated time - 30 min

b.
by taxi

Agree on price before entering the car! The maximum allowed price in Prague is 17 Kc/km. See taxi warning above!

cost - approx. 260 Kc with airport taxi (Belinda) or approx. 150 Kc with AAA, however you need to phone them 1080 and wait for them a few minutes on their meeting place on the right of the arrival hall right exit, estimated time - 10 min

2.
from Prague Main (Hlavni) railway station

a.
by public transport

entrance to the subway (Metro) is just inside the railway station hall; take the entrance closer to the outer windows of the big hall; take line C (red one) one station to the station "Muzeum"; change to subway line A (green one) - you go directly down from the middle of the platform - in the direction to "Dejvicka"; go to the station "Dejvicka"; take the exit by the first carriage of the subway train; one floor up take the right of two straight exits to trams; take tramway nr. 20 or 26 to the station "Koospol" in the direction "Divoka Sarka"; go straight back along the tracks 300 m and hotel Krystal is on your right (its photo is on the web); see map on the Web page.

you have to buy the ticket at the railway station before entering subway, either in automatic machines (you would need coins) or in a newspaper stand; buy a ticket for 12 Kc (valid for 60 min after stamping, in all city public transportation means); before entering the subway area, stamp it in yellow stamping machine in the big railway station hall

cost - 12 Kc, estimated time - 30-40 min

b.
by taxi

Agree on price before entering the car! The maximum allowed price in Prague is 17 Kc/km. See taxi warning above! cost - approx. 200 Kc with AAA, however you need to phone them 1080, estimated time - 20 min (might depend on traffic jam during working days)

3.
from Prague Holesovice railway station

a.
by public transport

from the railway station you enter the hall connecting it with subway station; turn right and very close is subway entrance; take line C (red one) to the station "Muzeum" and continue as described above for going from Prague Main railway station; you will pass through the subway station "Hlavni nadrazi" which is Prague Main railway station

you have to buy the ticket at the railway station before entering subway, either in automatic machines (you would need coins) or in a newspaper stand; buy a ticket for 12 Kc (valid for 60 min after stamping, in all city public transportation means); before entering the subway area stamp it in yellow stamping machine in the big railway station hall

cost - 12 Kc, estimated time - 35-45 min

b.
by taxi

Agree on price before entering the car! The maximum allowed price in Prague is 17 Kc/km. See taxi warning above!

cost - approx. 200 Kc with AAA, estimated time - 20 min (might depend on traffic jam during working days)

4.
arriving by car

Wherever you come from, take direction to the Airport or Chomutov; sooner or later this will bring you to the road nr. 7; if you are already on nr. 7 and are coming from center along the tramway tracks in the middle of the road - go until Esso gass station on your right, after the lights you go a bit down and see the hotel Krystal on your left; go to next lights where turn left, then left again and shortly you are in the front of the hotel; if you are on nr. 7 out of Prague going in the direction to center - go until you notice tramway ending loop on your left, after this notice tramway stops in the middle of the road, on the lights after the second stop turn right, then left and shortly you are in the front of the hotel. We suggest you to use either parking lot (40 Kc/day) or garage (70 Kc/day) of the Krystal hotel during your stay here.


Local public transport:

includes subway (Metro), tramways, buses and is working quite well; time-tables are on each stop; it works rather frequently over the day 6 am - 12 pm, some lines (not subway) are working over night basic fare for public transportation is 12 Kc and is valid 60 minutes; you might buy passes for 24 hours for 70 Kc, for 3 days for 180 Kc for 7 days for 250 Kc, these passes can be bought e.g. in the subway entrance under Jungman's square (Jungmanovo namesti) which is open 7 days a week
