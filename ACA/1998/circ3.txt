Registration:

   Registration will be on Saturday August 8 from 7:00 pm to 9:00 pm, 
   on Sunday August 9 from 8:00 am and then during the whole conference. 
   The conference starts on Sunday August 9 at 8:30 am by Welcome session.
   
   If possible please register on Saturday, we have many participants.
      

On site payments:

   On site payments will be accepted in Czech Crowns (Kc) in the actual
   exchange rate offered by the hotel Krystal and will be rounded to
   multiples of 50 Kc. Here are the on site fees at todays rate:
				  USD      Kc
	  IMACS member           $150     4,200
	  IMACS non-member       $170     4,750
	  student                $120     3,350
	  party                  $20        550
   Receipts will be issued in $(USD).
