ACA'98

1998 IMACS Conference on Applications of Computer Algebra

How much would it cost?

Note that all attendees, including organizers and speakers, will be responsible for all of their expenses including registration, travel, meals, and lodging. However, there is some modest support available.

Here we provide estimates of costs for participating in the conference:

  • conference fee (including 3 lunches, a party, e-mail access) is available on the registration form
  • hotel Krystal prices including breakfast are $25 for a single room, $34 for a double room occupied by two attendees and $45 for a double room occupied by an attendee and an accompanying person (for attendees we have a reasonable discount as this is an university hotel and the conference is organized by Czech Technical University), for other hotels check our suggestion.
  • as concerns other meals not included in the previous, you can have a nice dinner in Prague for $5-$10
  • local transportation: basic fare for public transportation is $0.40, for this you can go one way from/to airport, hotel, railway station, downtown; taxi for the same trip would cost $10-$20; you might also have a local transportation pass, 24 hours for $2, 3 days for $5.30 or 7 days for $7.40
  • you should estimate the cost of your ticket to Prague by yourself; you should arrive at Prague airport or one of railway stations: Praha Hlavni Nadrazi (Prague Main Station) or Praha Nadrazi Holesovice; detailed description how to reach the conference hotel will be provided later
So it seems that the conference will be quite cheap. We hope to have a lot of attendees, Prague is a nice city.
