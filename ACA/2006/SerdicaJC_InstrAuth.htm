Instructions for Authors

Language: The language of the journal is English.

Submission of manuscripts:

  • Preferably, manuscripts should be sent (either electronically in PDF or PostScript format or in hard copy, in triplicate) directly to a member of the Editorial Board whose field of interest is judged to be the closest to the subject of the paper. In case of hesitation authors are encouraged to consult the Editorial Office at the address computing@math.bas.bg. Manuscripts may also be submitted to the Editorial Office.
  • Upon acceptance an editable version of the paper, prepared by either TeX/LaTeX or Word, should be sent to the Editorial Office.

Guidelines for authors:

  • The affiliation, full address and email (if available) of each author should be indicated.
  • A self-contained abstract of no more than 150 words should be included at the beginning, stating the purposes of the study, procedures, findings and conclusions. After the abstract a list of index terms (a total of five to ten key words and phrases) should be given.
  • Footnotes should be numbered consecutively and placed at the foot of the page where they have to appear. The footnotes related to the title and author's names must not be numbered.
  • References to published literature should be quoted in text by an abbreviation in square brackets of name(s) (first three letters of single author; or first two letters of first author, followed by first letter of second author; or first letter of each author if there are three or more) and date (two digits). When more than one reference is cited with the same author(s) and date, the letters a, b, c, … should follow the date (eg [Smi88a], [Smi88b], etc.).
  • At the end of the paper all references should be listed alphabetically by author(s)' names, and then by the year if the same author has several papers, in the following format: a) to books—author, title, location, year of publication; b) to articles in periodicals or collections—author, title of the article, title of the periodical (collection), volume, year, page numbers.
  • When sending hard copy, author(s) should keep a copy of the original manuscript. Manuscripts will not be returned.

Proofs: Galley proofs are sent to the specified address for correspondence. For corrections (in the galley proofs) which exceed more than 10% of the text, the authors will be charged.

Offprints: Fifty offprints of each article will be provided free of charge.
