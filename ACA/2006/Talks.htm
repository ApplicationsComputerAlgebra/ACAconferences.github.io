How to submit a talk

The talks are always submitted to session organizers after the announcement of the proposed (and accepted) Special Sessions.

One who wants to submit a talk to a Special Session must send by e-mail the following information to a session organizer:

 

  • Title of the talk
  • Name(s) of the author(s)
  • An abstract of the talk.

 

The talk proposals will be reviewed by the session organizers and they will inform the authors about the acceptance.

If a proposal is accepted, the authors(s) must submit an abstract of the talk (11pt, 1/4 - 1/2 page of A4 paper, written in Latex) to the session organizer. See the sample in LaTeX format.

 

Deadline for Talk Proposals : May 31, 2006.
