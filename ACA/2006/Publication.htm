Publication of Presentations

 

A volume with abstracts of the talks presented at ACA 2006 will be published and will be available at the conference. The session organizers have to collect the abstracts of their sessions and to send them to Margarita Spiridonova till June 5, 2006.

 

The Local Arrangements Committee suggests the possibility full papers of some sessions to be published in special issues of Bulgarian journal Serdica Journal of Computing. These special issues will be published after the conference.

 

The session organizers could request for publishing papers presented at the corresponding sessions in Serdica Journal of Computing during the conference. The submissions of full papers can be done during the conference or some later (to be announced). For more details see the title page of Serdica Journal of Computing and Instruction for Authors.
