ACA 2006

12th International Conference

on Applications of Computer Algebra

        June 26 – 29, 2006, Varna, Bulgaria

 

 

The ACA Conferences are dedicated to reporting serious applications of symbolic computation (a.k.a. computer algebra) theories and tools for science, engineering and education. The 12th ACA Conference to be held in Varna, Bulgaria, on June 26-29, 2006, will continue this tradition.
