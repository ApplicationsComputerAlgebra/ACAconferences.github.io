Please use the following LaTeX templete for writing the abstracts.

The abstract should be sent as the main body of your email and "abstract"

should be typed in the subject.

 

% ABSTRACT TEMPLATE FOR ACA 2006

\documentstyle[11pt]{article}


\begin{document}


\title{Title of the Presentation to ACA 2006}
\author{Author(s)\\
lab\\
institution \\
city, country\\}


% DO NOT PUT ANYTHING FOR THE DATE.
% DO NOT MODIFY THE FOLLOWING THREE LINES.
\date{}
\maketitle
\thispagestyle{empty}


\begin{abstract}


% ABSTRACT BODY (ABOUT 1/2 OR 1/4 PAGES IN A4 FORMAT)

\end{abstract}
\end{document}
