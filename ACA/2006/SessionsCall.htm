Call for Special Sessions

 

The ACA meetings usually are organized as a series of Special Sessions.

The Proposals for Special Sessions for the ACA 2006 Conference have to be submitted to the Program Chairs of the Conference .

 

A Proposal for a Special Session has to include:

  • the title of the Special Session,
  • a short abstract describing the Special Session,
  • contact information of the organizers.

 

For more details see Instructions for Organizing a Special Session.

 

The International Advisory Committee will review the Proposals. The accepted Proposals will be announced on the Conference web page

 

Deadline for Special Sessions Proposals : April 30, 2006.
