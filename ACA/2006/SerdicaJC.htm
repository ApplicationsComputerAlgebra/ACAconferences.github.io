Serdica Journal of Computing

 

Recently a new Bulgarian quarterly scientific journal has been registered with the title Serdica Journal of Computing (Serdica is the ancient Thracian name of Sofia.). It is envisaged as a partner journal to Serdica Mathematical Journal (http://www.math.bas.bg/~serdica/),  which has been a quarterly publication of the Institute of Mathematics and Informatics at the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences for over 20 years.

The language of the publication is English. The first issue of Serdica Journal of Computing will be published in the spring of 2006. The title page and the instruction sheet might be seen.

 

It will be possible selected full papers of ACA 2006 Special Sessions to be published in special issues of Serdica Journal of Computing.
