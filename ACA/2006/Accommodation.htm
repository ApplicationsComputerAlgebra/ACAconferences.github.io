Accommodation

 

3-stars single and double rooms will be available for the period of ACA 2006 in the building of the International Home of Scientists (IHS) as well as in other 2, 3 and 4-stars hotels at walking distance to the IHS.

Hotel booking will be made by the Local Arrangements Committee. The conference participants are asked only to mark their wish for accommodation in the Registration Form.

Full board prices (including the costs of hotel and all meals from June 25th evening till June 30th morning):

  • Single room:
    • first category room - 370 €
    • second category room - 320 €
  • Bed in double room:
    • first category room - 295 €
    • second category room - 270 €

 

For participants wanting to stay additional days, suitable conditions will be arranged. After July 1st the hotel prices will be higher.
