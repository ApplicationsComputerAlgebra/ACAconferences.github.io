Enabling GRID for Computer Algebra Applications

Victor Edneral
Department of Theoretical High Energy Physics
Skobeltsyn Institute of Nuclear Physics, Moscow State University
Moscow, Russia


We briefly consider the EGEE (Enabling Grids for E - sciencE) project.  The
Grid here is a global system of spread calculations and of information
storage.  The described Grid net includes now about 180 resource centers all
over the world.  They use about $18,000$ CPUs with storage capacity ~10,200
TBytes as a whole.

Possibilities of usage of the Grid net on a base of Virtual Organizations (VO)
are considered.  A perspective of creation of the VO "calg" for a computer
algebra applications usage is discussed.
