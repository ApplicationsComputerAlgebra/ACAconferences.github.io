Application of Computer Algebra Systems for Obtaining, Illustration and
Verification of Mathematical Results

Anna Tomova
Department of Mathematics and Informatics
Naval Academy "N. J. Vapcarov"
Varna, Bulgaria

The contemporary Computer algebra Systems (CAS), such as Mathematica, Maple,
Reduce, Matlab and others, have a large scale of capabilities allowing them to
be used for automatic performance and illustration of mathematical
computations.  These systems can be used for obtaining and verification of
mathematical results as well, thus helping the mathematical developments.

The CAS should be wider used at all levels of education in mathematics.  Some
Bulgarian universities have good experience in this field.  In particular,
successful application of CAS in education of mathematics at Varna Naval
Academy has made.  Some examples of such application of CAS are described as
well as their use via Internet.  CAS applications for obtaining and
verification of mathematical results are also considered.
