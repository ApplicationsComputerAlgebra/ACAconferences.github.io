The Interaction of Computer Algebra Systems and Course Management Systems in
Teaching Business Calculus at the American College of Thessaloniki

Panos Vlachos, Ph.D Chair, Division of Technology & Science,
American College of Thessaloniki

Emmanuel Maou, Associate Professor,  Division of Technology & Science,
American College of Thessaloniki


In this talk we present the advantages of the joint use of computer algebra
systems and course management systems in teaching Business Calculus at the
American College of Thessaloniki.  This is part of a project funded by the
Andrew Mellon foundation whose aim is to implement these technologies in the
educational process.

The talk presents our classroom experience and focuses on:

The use of the Computer Algebra system (MathCAD) in applying the Harvard
Calculus Reform approach.

The use of the Course Management System (Blackboard) in the organization of the
course, the delivery of instructional material, student formal assessment and
student's self-assessment.

The integration of the two technologies to improve student learning and mastery
of the course material.
