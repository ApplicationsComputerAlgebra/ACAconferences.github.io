Format of the ACA meetings

 

The ACA meetings usually are organized as a series of Special Sessions.

 

Proposals for Special Sessions and other scientific activities at the ACA 2006 Conference have to be submitted to the Program Chairs of the Conference.

 

For more details see Call for Special Sessions and Instructions for Organizing a Special Session.

 

Talk proposals are always sent to Session organizers (for more details see How to submit a talk).
