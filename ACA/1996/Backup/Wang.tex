{\bf Description}
\medskip

This special session focuses on applying classical and modern elimination algorithms and tools to
problems in various domains of science, engineering and industry. Some already successful
applications (for example, in mathematical computation, geometric reasoning/modeling/design,
system/control theory, computer vision/robotics and neural networks) will be surveyed and
reported, and still un-attackable problems are to be identified for further research and as challenge
to future improvement and development of elimination algorithms and their implementation.
