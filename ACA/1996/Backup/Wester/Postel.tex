\begin{center}
\large
Abstract
\end{center}

Using a wide set of more than 50 different kinds of ordinary differential equations and systems,
we try to give an idea of the capabilities of Axiom, Derive, Macsyma, Maple, Mathematica,
MuPAD and Reduce in solving differential equations. In our conclusion we firstly want to give the
user an answer to the question "What system should I use?" and secondly to point out to developers
of computer algebra systems the advantages and/or drawbacks of their ODE solver, to help to
improve it.
