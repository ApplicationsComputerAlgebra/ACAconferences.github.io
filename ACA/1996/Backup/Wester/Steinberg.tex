Abstract
\end{center}

Problem Solving Environments provide a promising new approach for solving modeling problems
that occur in engineering and science. Such environments will provide easy access to integrated
symbolic and numeric computing and this will greatly enhance the tools available to modelers.
However, this will place new demands on computer algebra systems.
