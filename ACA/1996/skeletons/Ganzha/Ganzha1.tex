\begin{center}
\large
Abstract
\end{center}

A general vector-matrix algorithm has been proposed and implemented with Mathematica for
obtaining the characteristic equation of a difference scheme on curvilinear grids approximating the
Euler equations. All the known types (C,O,H) of curvilinear grids around the airfolis have been
considered and it is shown how the grid topology affects the stability of difference schemes.
