Implicitization of Nested Circular Curves and generalizations

Hoon Hong, Josef Schicho

Date: July 18th (Thursday)
Time: 09:00-09:30
Abstract
A nested circular curve is a plane curve traced by a point on a circle that rotates around another circle that again rotates around still another circle, and so on. In this talk, we give an efficient method for obtaining an implicit equation of a nested circular curve. Further we show how to generalize the result to case of nested elipses (a joint work with Josef Schicho).
