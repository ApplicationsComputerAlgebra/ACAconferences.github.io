Application of Groebner basis in constraint of non-numerical domains

Yosuke Sato

Date: July 19th (Friday)
Time: 08:30-08:55
Abstract
We discuss Groebner bases in a polynomial ring over a Boolean ring which is a commutative ring but not an integral domain. We prove they have several nice features such as easily constructed comprehensive Groebner bases. Using them we can provide constraint solvers over non-numerical domains of sets.
