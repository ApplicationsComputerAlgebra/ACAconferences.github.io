Computer Algebra and Problem Solving Environments

Stanly Steinberg

Date: July 20th (Saturday)
Time: 11:00-11:22.5
Abstract
Problem Solving Environments provide a promising new approach for solving modeling problems that occur in engineering and science. Such environments will provide easy access to integrated symbolic and numeric computing and this will greatly enhance the tools available to modelers. However, this will place new demands on computer algebra systems.
