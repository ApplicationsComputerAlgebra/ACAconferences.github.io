\begin{center}
\large
Abstract
\end{center}

The goal of the project is to develop and experiment algorithms, allowing the integration of
algebraic and numerical tools for devising efficient algorithms for the solution of systems of
polynomial equations. I will quickly present two components of the project, which are the software
integration part and the algorithmic part. Applications of theses tools will be given, connected
with concrete problems coming from robotics, vision, scheduling, signal processing (?)...
