Official Airline for PASCO'97/ISSAC'97/IMACS-ACA'97

United Airlines is the official airline for the three computer algebra conferences being held in Maui. United flies into Kahului airport (OGG), a short drive from the Aston Wailea Resort. As official airline, United provides the following discounts for travel from the United States, Puerto Rico and Canada for travel from July 10th through August 16th, 1997:
  • 5% off United's lowest published excursion fare
  • 5% off business and first class fares
  • 10% off unrestricted full fare coach with 7 day advanced purchase
These discounts apply for travel solely on United and United Express. Note that Aloha Airlines, one of the major interisland carriers in Hawaii, codeshares many flights with United (that is, many Aloha flights are listed also as United flights).

For non-U.S./Canadian participants, the above discounts are not available, but if it is to your advantage to fly United, we encourage you to book through the avenues listed below as this will provide benefits to the meetings.

To take advantage of these United discounts, you or your travel agent can contact either the United Meeting Desk [(800) 521-4041] or International Tours and Cruises (see below), tell them you are associated with the PASCO/ISSAC/ACA meetings and mention the code letters 503WP. Note that the tickets need to be issued by either United or International Tours and Cruises to obtain these discounts.
