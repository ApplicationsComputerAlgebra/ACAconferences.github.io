\centerline{\bf Moving Coframes}

\vskip 0.3cm

\centerline{Peter Olver}
\centerline{Department of Mathematics}
\centerline{University of Minnesota}
\centerline{Minneapolis, MN 55455, USA}
\centerline{Email: olver@ima.umn.edu}

\vskip 0.3cm

In this talk I will present a new approach to the theory of
moving frames that allows one to explicitly compute them
for arbitrary Lie group and pseudo-group actions.
Applications to differential invariants, equivalence, and
computer vision, as well as computer algebra implementation
issues will be discussed.
