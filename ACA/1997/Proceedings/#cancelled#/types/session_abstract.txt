Session on _Types and Specification_ at IMACS-ACA
Jacques Calmet (calmet@ira.uka.de)

The IMACS-ACA'97 (Applications of Computer Algebra) meeting will be held at the
Aston Wailea Resort, Maui, Hawaii on July 24-26, 1997. For information see:
	http://math.unm.edu/ACA/1997.html

The session on types and specification addresses the problem of how complete
a typing system can be.  The topic of this session is not just applications to
Computer Algebra but applications of typing and specifying into mathematical
symbolic computing.  Another goal is to bring together experts of typing in
programming languages, lambda calculi, specifications and computer algebra
to make this session an interdisciplinary workshop.  In addition, talks
describing the typing mechanisms in presently available computer algebra
systems are strongly encouraged.

To be invited to give a talk in this session, send a title and abstract to 
the session organizer (and program co-chair) at
	calmet@ira.uka.de
There will not be printed proceedings. Accepted papers will appear
electronically in the URL of the conference.  As done in the past, if this
session is as outstanding as I hope, then the papers will be published as a
special issue of a journal.
