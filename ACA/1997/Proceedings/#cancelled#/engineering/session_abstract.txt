The use of computer algebra provides a potential solution to a variety of
problems in engineering and algorithm design.  The session is concerned with
applications of computer algebra, e.g., in the following fields: automatic
generation of algorithms and architectures, algebraic methods for error control
coding and modulation, formal methods in industrial system engineering and
control, algorithms for quantum and optical computing, computer algebra for
digital signal and image processing (signal transforms, pattern recognition,
compression, coding, etc.).
