     Mathpert: A symbolic computation program designed for learning algebra,
     trigonometry, and calculus.

     Mathpert is a symbolic calculation and graphing program designed for
     students and teachers. It allows users to produce, and can also produce
     by itself, step-by-step solutions of problems in algebra, trigonometry,
     and one-variable calculus. It adheres to the following principles:
     correctness, transparency, cognitive fidelity. Correctness means strict
     logical correctness: unlike other symbolic computation programs, there
     is a powerful logical apparatus which guards against erroneous
     applications of computational operations. Correctness also applies to
     graphs: the singularities, jumps, and extrema are symbolically
     calculated so that graphs are scrupulously correct.
     Transparency, or the "glass-box" principle, means that you can see how
     the program achieved its results. It produces step-by-step solutions
     with comprehensible steps, not just a one-line answer. Cognitive
     fidelity means that it produces solutions as an experienced human
     would, using student-level algorithms rather than powerful but obscure
     algorithms. The power of the closely interwoven symbolic and logical
     machinery is made available to help students learn through an
     innovative user interface.
