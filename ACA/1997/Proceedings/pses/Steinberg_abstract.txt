Title:
The Use of Algorithm Templates in a Problem Solving Environment for the
Numerical Solution of Differential Equations

Authors: R. L. Akers, E. Kant, C. J. Randall, S. Steinberg*, R. L. Young

Abstract:
SciNapse is a problem solving environment that transforms high-level
descriptions of initial boundary value problems for systems of partial
differential equations to efficient, documented and executable code, typically
C or Fortran.  Modelers can concisely specify problems at a level that mirrors
the mathematical description of the problem.  This talk will provide an
overview of SciNapse and then gives more detailed discussions of the use of
algorithm templates for representing algorithms, and how the templates are
translated into executable code.
