\begin{center}
{\bf
VECTOR REPRESENTATIONS OF INVOLUTIVE DIVISIONS
       AND SOME NEW INVOLUTIVE DIVISIONS
}\\
\vspace {0.3cm}
{Yu-Fu Chen}\\
{\it \small Institute of System Science, Academia Sinica, 100080 Beijing,
P.R. China \\
e-mail: yfchen@mmrc.iss.ac.cn}
\end{center}
\vspace {0.6cm}
This talk contains two parts. First, a vector representation
of the general involutive division proposed by Gerdt and Blinkov was given.
Using this representation, most of the properties of involutive divisions
such as Noetherity, Artinity and Constructivity, can be simplified. Second,
Thomas and Janet divisions were generalized to two types of involutive
divisions, and new involutive divisions were found. Also the involutive
completion algorithm on monomials given by Gerdt et al was improved.
