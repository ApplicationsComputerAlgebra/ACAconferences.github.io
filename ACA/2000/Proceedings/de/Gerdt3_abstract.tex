\begin{center}
{\bf
COMPUTATION OF JANET BASES. II.POLYNOMIAL BASES}\\
\vspace {0.3cm}
{Vladimir Gerdt$^1$, Yuri Blinkov$^2$, Denis Yanovich$^1$}\\
{\it \small$^1$Laboratory of Computing Techniques and Automation, Joint Institute for Nuclear
Research, 141980 Dubna, Russia, e-mail: gerdt@jinr.ru\ \ yan@cv.jinr.ru \\
$^2$Department of Mathematics and Mechanics, Saratov University, 410071 Saratov, Russia
\\ e-mail: BlinkovUA@info.sgu.ru}
\end{center}
\vspace {1.0cm}
Involutive bases of polynomial ideals~\cite{GB} and linear differential
ideals~\cite{G99} are Gr\"{o}bner bases of special form. Though involutive
bases are generally redundant as Gr\"{o}bner ones, their use makes
more accessible the structural information of polynomial and
differential ideals. Janet bases may be cited as typical representatives
of involutive bases and have been used in algebraic and Lie symmetry
analysis of differential equations. In this talk we present the first
versions of programs in C, C++ and Reduce implementing the involutive
algorithms for construction of Janet bases of polynomial ideals. We compare the
timings for these programs with those for computer algebra system
{\em Singular} running on the same computer platform. As benchmarks
we consider those collected in~\cite{BM} and have been widely used for
Gr\"{o}bner bases software.
