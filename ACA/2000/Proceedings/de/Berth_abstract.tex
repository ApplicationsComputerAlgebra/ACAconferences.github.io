\begin{center}
{\bf
SOLVING ORDINARY DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS BY EQUIVALENCE TRANSFORMATIONS
}\\
\vspace {0.3cm}
{Matthias Berth}\\
{\it \small Institut f\"{u}r Mathematik und Informatik, Universit\"{a}t Greifswald,
17487 Greifswald, Germany \\
e-mail: berth@mail.uni-greifswald.de}
\end{center}
\vspace {0.6cm}
We present an effective algorithmic method for characterizing
equivalence classes of ODEs and for the computation of equivalence
transformations. For second order ODEs, one can use invariants which
were found by Arthur Tresse in 1896. We will illustrate the method for
Emden-Fowler equations $y''=A x^n y^m$, showing how certain second order
ODEs that are equivalent to Emden-Fowler equations with known solution
can be solved algorithmically.
