On the Use of Derive and MathCad When Training Teachers of Mathematics

The research conducted by the author has shown that many teachers of
mathematics would like to use computer at their lessons but they do not
consider themselves to be ready for it.  One of important goals of teachers of
teachers training colleges is training future teachers for the use of computers
in their work with pupils.  But the majarity of mathematics teachers have but
vague idea of opportunities of using computers when teaching mathematics.  The
author has experience of conducnting special seminars on the work in Derive
system and special seminar "Mathematics and Computer" where, in particular, the
issues of application of Mathcad system by mathematics teachers were
considered.  The special seminar in Derive system was conducted with 2nd year
students.  The major attention was paid at solving mathematical analysis
problems.  The opportunities of applaying Derive system when teaching pupils
and students are analyzed in the book of the author of the report which is
titled "Practicum on solving problems
