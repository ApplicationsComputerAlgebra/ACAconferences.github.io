This session is intended to discuss Computer Algebra methods and = algorithms=20 in the study of Dynamical Systems.
The session will also focus on = important=20 applications of Computer Algebra to Dynamical Systems arising in many =
areas=20 of science and engineering.=20

Since nonlinear Dynamical Systems cannot be exactly solved in = general, the=20 role of Computer Algebra in finding
approximate solutions as well as = in the=20 pre-analysis for the numerical methods, is extremely important. From = this=20
point of view, the construction of exact or approximate solutions in = symbolic form constitutes the most powerful
approach to study the = behavior=20 of Dynamical Systems. Computer Algebra methods have also emerged as = powerful=20
tools in investigating stability and bifurcations.=20

This meeting is advertized in the INRIA, CADC and = ADS = servers.

  • Construction of normal forms.=20
