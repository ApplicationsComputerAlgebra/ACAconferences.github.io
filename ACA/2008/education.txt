   Overview:

   Education has become one of the fastest growing application areas for
   computers in general and computer algebra in particular. Computer
   algebra tools such as TI-Nspire CAS, TI Voyage 200, TI-89, Axiom, Derive,
   Maple, Maxima, Mathematica, MuPAD, or Reduce, make powerful teaching
   tools in mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, economy, etc.

   The goal of this session is to exchange ideas and experiences, to hear
   about classroom experiments, and to discuss all issues related with
   the use of computer algebra tools in classroom (such as assessment,
   new teaching and learning styles, change of curricula, new support material,
   ...)

   If you are interested in presenting a paper, please contact one of the
   organizers.
