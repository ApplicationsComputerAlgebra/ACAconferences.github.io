A Symbolic-Numeric Approach to Minimal Polynomial Logic and to Rule Based
Expert Systems that Use this Logic

E. Roanes-Lozano, Luis M. Laita and E. Roanes-Macias

We had previously developed an algebraic model of classic Boolean and modal
many-valued logics.  A similar approach, also using computer algebra techniques
(Groebner bases) and oriented to perform effective calculus in a certain
continuous logic: minimal polynomial logic (MPL), and also to perform knowledge
extraction and to check consistency in rule based expert systems (RBES) whose
underlying logic is MPL, is presented.  The implementation has been developed
in the computer algebra system Maple.  The presentation will be illustrated
with examples of simple RBES.
