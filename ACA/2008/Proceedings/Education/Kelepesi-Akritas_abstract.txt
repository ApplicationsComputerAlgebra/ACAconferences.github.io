Techniques for Translating Between the Equivalent Representations of Polynomial
Functions with the TI-Nspire CAS Technology

Eugenia Kelepesi-Akritas, Aristotelis Raptis, Athanasia Raptis and
Alkiviadis G. Akritas

In the scientific community it is widely accepted that for a deeper
understanding of the concept of function students need to be able to translate
between the three most commonly used representations of polynomial functions,
that is, between the algebraic expression (A), tabular form (T) and graphical
representation (G).

In this talk we demonstrate the modeling capabilities of the computer algebra
system TI-Nspire CAS and show how to perform the translations A <-> T, T <-> G,
G <-> A, completing thus the Triple Representation Model (TRM) by Schwarz,
Dreyfus & Bruckheimer.
