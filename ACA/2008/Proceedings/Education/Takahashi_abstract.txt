Computer Algebra Systems and Representations

Tadashi Takahashi
Graduate School of Human Development and Environment
Kobe University, Kobe, Japan

Many proposals have already been made with regard to the effective use of
computer algebra systems in mathematics education.  If we hope to develop and
improve what has already been achieved, we will need to take advantage of the
research results from cognitive science.  Computer algebra systems are powerful
tools that allow learners to _run_ mathematical formulae.  Powerful tools can
become effective tools by finding effective ways to use them.  In order to
fully utilize computer algebra systems in mathematics education, teaching
methods that are based on cognitive science will be needed, and these are
clearly different from those used in _pen and paper_ teaching.  This paper
considers the use of computer algebra systems as _tools_ and analyzes how such
tools can affect the thinking of learners.

We report the practices in university mathematics by using computer algebra
system.
