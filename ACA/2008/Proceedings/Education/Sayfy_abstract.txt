     Numerical Algorithm for the Order Verification of Numerical Methods


                Sayfy                              S. Khoury

               sayfy@aus.edu                  skhoury@aus.edu


                                  Abstract

In this  paper,  we  have  introduced  a  unified  approach  for  the  order
verification of the errors in  Taylor  polynomial  approximations  and  many
numerical methods. An  algorithm,  based  on  the  computer  algebra  system
"MAPLE, is written and tested by  verifying  the  error  for  many  familiar
numerical methods that arise  in  the  undergraduate  curriculum,  such  as,
Taylor polynomial approximation, Newton's method for solving equations,  the
5-point forward numerical differentiation  formula,  the  composite  Simpson
rule, the 4-stage explicit Runge-Kutta  method,  and  the  3-point  Gaussian
integration formula.

The algorithm offers a simple and unified way to understand and  verify  the
error of Taylor approximations as well as numerical  methods.   Thus  it  is
convenient  for  teaching  purposes   in   the   undergraduate   mathematics
curriculum.
