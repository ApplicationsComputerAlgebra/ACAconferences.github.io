Support for students and early carrer researchers

    There are two types of support we offer


  • Accommodation

    Accommodation in the KYK dormitory. The dormitory is 15km from the SCALE location. There will be transportation from and to the conference venue every day. We offer accommodation in triple bed rooms. Everyone staying in the dormitory must be vaccinated and fulfill all the COVID related requirement that will apply at the time of the conference.

    To apply, fill in this form.

  • Travel grant

    We have applied to various sources for funding that will cover a limited amount of travel grants. Priority will be given to students and early stage researchers from developing countries and/or women.

    To apply, fill in this form.

    • Other types of support, e.g., waving the registration fee, is also possible under special circumstances.
