Traveling to ACA'2001

2001 Conference on Applications of Computer Algebra

To travel to ACA'2001, you will need to fly into Albuquerque International Airport (ABQ). To get from the airport to the conference hotels, use the courtesy phone in the arrivals area to call your hotel and have them pick you up. The hotels will also provide a complimentary shuttle back to the airport. Free bus service between the conference hotels and the conference site on the TVI main campus (see map and more detailed map) will be provided. TVI is located one mile north of the conference hotels (which are located near Gibson and University) and is about 1/2 mile south of a main restaurant area near the University of New Mexico campus. A rental car will not be required.
