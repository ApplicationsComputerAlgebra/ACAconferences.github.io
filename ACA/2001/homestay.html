ACA'2001 Home Stay Possibilities

In addition, some inexpensive rooms will be available in the homes of local people. If you are interested in such an accommodation, please send a note to ACA and we will attempt to make an informal match between you and one of our local volunteers. We cannot guarantee anything (this matching will be done soley on a case by case basis), but the idea is that we will put you in contact with one of our volunteers (if one is available) and let you two negotiate directly on how you want to work things out. The number of people that we can accept is not yet known (it depends on how many volunteers we have), but we will try to respond in a timely manner once we get it all going.
