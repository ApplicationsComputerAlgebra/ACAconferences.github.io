ACA'2001 Financial Support

2001 Conference on Applications of Computer Algebra

Note that all attendees, including organizers and speakers, will be responsible for all of their expenses including registration, travel, meals and lodging. However, there is some modest support available. Applications should be sent to aca@math.unm.edu and should include requested amount and purpose. Preferences will be given to students, junior faculty, organizers and speakers. Local expenses and meals will not be covered (however, most meals will be provided with the conference). Note that requests for partial support are much more likely to be funded than requests to have all expenses paid.
