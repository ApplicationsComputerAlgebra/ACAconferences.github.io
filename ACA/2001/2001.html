The ACA meetings are organized as a series of Special Sessions. To propose a special session, please send
  • the names of the organizers,
  • email addresses of the organizers,
  • the title of the special session,
  • a short abstract describing the special session,
  • and a list of proposed speakers
to the chair of the Scientific Committee, Stanly Steinberg (see the instructions for Organizing a Special Session). The Scientific Committee will review the proposal. Selected papers will be considered for publication in the Journal of Symbolic Computation.
