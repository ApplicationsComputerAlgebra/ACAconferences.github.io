The Applications of Computer Algebra Working Group (ACAWG) and Scientific Committee is responsible for the organization the Applications of Computer Algebra (ACA) meetings. In particular, the ACAWG will choose the place for future meetings. As the Scientific Committee, this group will approve all scientific activities at the ACA meetings. The format of each ACA meeting is controlled by the Organizers, but usually the meetings will consist of special session. Proposals for special sessions and other scientific activities should be submitted to the chair, Stanly Steinberg, of the Working Group who will ask the Working Group for comments before approving the activity. Most activities will be approved, but the Working Group may and should recommend improvements.
