Session title:

"Computer algebra in analysis and solving of equations in
 mathematical physics and control theory"

The goal of the session is to provide a forum for developers of
constructive algebraic methods and software for
equations and systems of interest in mathematical physics and
control theory and for those who need them to investigate and
solve particular equations.

The session topics include:

- group analysis of differential, integral and difference equations

- symmetries of solutions of boundary value problem for the above mentioned
  equations (such symmetries are well-known as renorm-group symmetries)

- blow-up regime and power solutions

- linear systems of partial differential equations

- integrability analysis of nonlinear evolution equations

- completion of polynomial and differential systems to involution

- computations with quantum algebras and groups

- ordinary and partial control systems

- constrained dynamics and differential-algebraic equations

Session organizers:

Vladimir Gerdt, Vladimir Kovalev and Dmitry Shirkov
