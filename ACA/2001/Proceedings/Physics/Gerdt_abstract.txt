Constraint Hamiltonian Dynamics and Involutive Bases

Vladimir Gerdt (JINR, Dubna, Russia)

In this talk we apply the algorithmic techniques of commutative computer
algebra based on the use of involutive polynomial bases to finite-dimensional
constrained Hamiltonian systems of polynomial type.  We show that involutive
bases allows one to compute the complete set of constraints and separate them
into the first and second classes in accordance to the Dirac classification.
