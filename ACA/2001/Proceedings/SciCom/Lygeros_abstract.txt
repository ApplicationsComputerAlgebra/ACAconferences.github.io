Computation of Erdos-Woods Numbers

Nik Lygeros

Erdos-Woods Numbers are defined as the length of an interval of consecutive
integers whose every element is not coprime with its extremeties.  Woods was
the first to find such numbers, Dowe proved there exists an infinity and
Cagielski, Heroult and Richard that their set is recursive.  Our aim is to
study the arithmetical proprieties of those numbers.
