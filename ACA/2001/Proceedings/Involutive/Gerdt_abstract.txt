Title: Fast computation of polynomial Janet bases

Author: Gerdt, Vladimir P. (in cooperation with Yu. A. Blinkov and
        D. A. Yanovich)

Affiliation: JINR Dubna, Russia

Abstract: In this talk we present basic algorithmic ideas and their
implementation in Reduce, C and C++ for computation of Janet bases for
polynomial ideals.  We also give the timings of our codes in comparison with
the latest version (2-0-0) of computer algebra system Singular having one of
the most efficient implementations of the Buchberger algorithm for computation
of Groebner bases.
