Title: Differential Elimination Algorithms and Deformations of Symmetric
       Differential Systems

Author: Greg Reid
www.orrca.on.ca/~reid
Greg.Reid@uwo.ca

Affiliation: University of London, Ontario

Abstract: 

Novel approaches, specialized C-implementations, probabilistic and classical
symbolic differential elimination algorithms are discussed.  Determination of
approximate symmetry groups and algebraic-geometric properties of differential
systems are also discussed.
