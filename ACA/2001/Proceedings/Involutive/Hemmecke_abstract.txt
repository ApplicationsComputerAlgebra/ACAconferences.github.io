Title:  Dynamical Aspects of Involutive Bases Computations

Author: Hemmecke, Ralf 

Affiliation: RISC, University of Linz, Austria

Abstract:

In this talk we give a short introduction to involutive divisions and
involutive bases computations.  Different approaches are discussed and compared
and a new class of involutive divisions is presented.  We show results of our
computer experiments with the different approaches.
