  Title:        Symbolic Computation and Wavelets
  Authors:      Peter Paule
  Affiliation:  RISC-Linz, J. Kepler University, Linz, Austria
  Abstract:
The talk reports on recent work joint with F. Chyzak, O. Scherzer, A.
Schoisswohl and B. Zimmermann.  It discusses the construction of wavelets in
connection with the use of methods from computer algebra, in particular,
Gr{\"o}bner bases and symbolic summation.
