2001 IMACS-ACA (Applications of Computer Algebra) Conference
31 May - 3 June 2001, Albuquerque, New Mexico, USA




Special Session:
Symbolic and Numerical Scientific Computation


Session Chair:
Franz Winkler (RISC Linz)



Abstract:

There are basically two major approaches to scientific computation, namely a symbolic approach and a numerical approach. Both have their advantages and disadvantages, and the two approaches have led to rather disjoint scientific communities. In this session we will bring together researchers from both communities and try to bridge the gap.

Call for Submissions:

If you want to present a talk which combines both the aspects in the topic then please send the abstract to Franz Winkler, before March 31, 2001.
