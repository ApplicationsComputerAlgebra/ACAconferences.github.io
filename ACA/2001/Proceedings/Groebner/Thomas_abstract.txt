Title:    Toric Initial Ideals without Embedded Primes

Speaker:  Rekha R. Thomas, Math, University of Washington, Seattle

In this talk, we give characterizations and examples of toric initial ideals
without embedded primes.  Such ideals give rise to families of integer programs
in which every program can be solved by the classical Gomory group
relaxations.

Joint work with Serkan Hosten.
