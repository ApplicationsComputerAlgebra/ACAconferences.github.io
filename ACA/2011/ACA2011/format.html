ACA Meeting Format

The meeting is run in a standard format where sessions are held in one or more 2 to 3-hour blocks of time. These blocks typically consist of half-hour talks or a one-hour overview and half-hour talks. The half-hour slot includes time for questions.

Proposing a session:


Individuals are invited to organize a session. For examples of past sessions, consult past ACA conferences. Proposals should be sent to the conference Program Chairs, who will review proposals in collaboration with the ACAWG. The duties of the sessions organizers are:

  • Submit a proposal for the session to qntran@lamar.edu.

  • Invite the speakers.

  • Maintain a web page describing the session and providing talk abstracts.

Each session organizing team will issue a call for papers/contributions stating the terms for submitting contributions, including the deadline each team has set for receiving.
