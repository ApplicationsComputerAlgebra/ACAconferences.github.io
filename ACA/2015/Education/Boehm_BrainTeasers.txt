Solving Brain Teasers/Twisters - CAS Assisted

Josef Bohm, Austria

Most of brain teasers can be faced using Mathematical Logic procedures.
Therefore, using a CAS, which handles with Logic expressions, may help solving
this kind of problems.  The German weekly newspaper Die Zeit offers every week
another brain teaser.  Just for fun I tried to solve one or the other problem
assisted by CAS.  I will apply equations, Boolean expressions and other tools
in order to solve the problems.  It is a special challenge to produce own brain
teasers.

The focus will not be directed on programming skills but on the mathematization
process of the problems.

The software chosen for the presentation is my old and good friend DERIVE.  I
will also use TI-NspireCAS but the procedures presented can be transferred to
all other CAS, of course.
