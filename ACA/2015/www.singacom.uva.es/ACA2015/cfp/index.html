Presentation and dates

Presentation

As it was announced at ACA 2015 there will be a volume of the Springer Proceedings in Mathematics & Statistics series devoted to ACA 2015. For the volume we welcome full versions of papers of topics related to those listed at any of the Special Sessions. Individual contributions will be all refereed to the high quality standards of leading journals in the field.

Dates (hard deadline)

  • Submission deadline: November 27, 2015
  • Acceptation/rejection date: April, 2016
Instructions
  • Paper submission will be handled via the Springer Online Conference Service (OCS).
  • Each submitted paper should adhere to the guidelines to present an appropriate level of uniformity. You must use the following LaTeX template.
  • Each corresponding author is required to sign the authorization to publish form, and return it to the Editors of the volume by e-mail.
About the Springer Proceedings in Mathematics & Statistics series

This book series features volumes composed of select contributions from workshops and conferences in all areas of current research in mathematics and statistics, including operation research and optimization. In addition to an overall evaluation of the interest, scientific quality, and timeliness of each proposal at the hands of the publisher, individual contributions are all refereed to the high quality standards of leading journals in the field. Thus, this series provides the research community with well-edited, authoritative reports on developments in the most exciting areas of mathematical and statistical research today.
